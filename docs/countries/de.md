# Germany / Netherlands / France (EU) — market profile

Date 2026-09-25. Part of [market-selection.md](market-selection.md). **Verdict: large but hardest compliance in Dec 2026; watchlist.**

## Demand
- Germany is Europe's largest furniture consumer market (~EUR 38.6B, Furnilytics; USD 21.2B, Mordor - ESTIMATE, definitions differ). France specialist furniture retail EUR 19.9B (ESTIMATE). https://www.furnilytics.com/indicators/retail/market-size/europe-furniture-consumer-market-size-by-country/
- Shares of Indian handicraft exports FY25: Germany 5.37%, Netherlands 5.09%, France 3.03% (IBEF) = 13.5% combined - VERIFIED. Germany, Netherlands, France also in EPCH's top woodware list (led by USA, Germany, Netherlands, UK, France).
- Netherlands is partly a re-distribution hub (seair - ESTIMATE).

## Duties / compliance
- EUDR: wooden furniture (HS 9403) applies **30 Dec 2026** to all sizes; India low risk; simplified due diligence but traceability/geolocation still needed. https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/en/news/delay-until-december-2026-and-other-developments-implementation-eudr-regulation
- EU-India FTA concluded 27 Jan 2026 (furniture tariffs cut up to 10.5% claimed - ESTIMATE), not ratified; rollout ~2027. Current MFN furniture duty not verified.
- From 1 Jul 2026 EUR 3 per item flat duty on parcels < EUR 150 through 1 Jul 2028; VAT via IOSS (standard rates ~19% DE, ~21% NL, ~20% FR - ASSUMPTION, verify). GPSR/ product safety and GDPR obligations (ASSUMPTION).
- CITES sheesham > 10 kg permit; EU importer needs CITES import handling.
