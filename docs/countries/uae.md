## Duties / compliance
- 5% GCC duty + 5% VAT; India-UAE CEPA zero duty on ~80% of lines from day 1 (May 2022), needs certificate of origin (DGFT). https://www.moet.gov.ae/en/cepa_india
- UAE: no product-level certificate for standard furniture (secondary source). Saudi: 15% duty + SABER conformity certificate (secondary) - keep Saudi out of scope.
- CITES > 10 kg sheesham permit applies at export.
