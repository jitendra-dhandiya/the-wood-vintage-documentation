## Headline findings (as of 2026-09-25)
1. Tariff picture has moved a lot in 2026 (FAST). Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs (Learning Resources v. Trump, 2026-02-20). Section 122 10% surcharge ran 2026-02-24 to 2026-07-24. From 2026-07-24 a Section 301 "forced-labor" tariff applies; India is in the flat 10% group (law-firm sources, VERIFIED secondary). Section 232 25% still applies to upholstered wooden furniture and kitchen cabinets/vanities (increase to 50% delayed to 2027-01-01). Plain non-upholstered wood furniture and decor are NOT under 232 timber duties (ASSUMPTION - confirm HTS by HTS with broker).
2. De minimis ($800) is gone for all countries; CBP interim final rules of 2026-06-24 suspend it indefinitely (statutory repeal 2027-07-01). Every DTC parcel needs an entry with duty. So "DDP" is now a real process, not a label.
3. Wood legality is the highest-consequence area: Lacey Act declaration (species + harvest country) is required for wood furniture/decor entries; sheesham (Dalbergia sissoo) sits under CITES Appendix II via the Dalbergia genus listing with Annotation #15. Exact exemption wording for finished goods and any CoP20 change could NOT be verified - get written confirmation from the India CITES Management Authority/EPCH before shipping any sheesham item.
4. India courier exports: the Rs 10 lakh per-consignment cap was removed from 2026-04-01 (DGFT Notification 67/2025-26; CBIC Circular 17/2026-Customs) - VERIFIED via secondary source.
5. Cross-border payments: RBI Master Direction on Payment Aggregators (2025-09-15) covers PA-CB; per-transaction cap Rs 25 lakh (secondary source). Etsy Payments in India is via Payoneer (secondary).
