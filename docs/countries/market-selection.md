Domestic size for context (ESTIMATE, sources disagree): India furniture USD 25-31B, home décor USD 25.5B; US furniture USD 180-196B.
India is by far the biggest *addressable-to-us* market by volume and the only one we can serve today.

### Recommendation (decision-oriented)

1. **Primary market for the first 100 orders: India.** It is the only market where the platform is already live
   (decisions 0027, 0031: online payment blocked for non-INR), COD works, delivery is days not weeks, no CITES/Lacey/EUDR
   burden, and returns/damage are handled in-country. Speed-to-100 is limited by marketing (0 posts today), not compliance.
   Honest downside: India is the *most price-competitive* market (Wooden Street lists sheesham king beds at INR 24,999-58,999
   after 45-62% "discounts" — VERIFIED 2026-09-25) and COD return-to-origin is high (see `in.md`).
2. **Secondary market (Phase 2 research, pilot after order #30-50): USA — but as a hybrid**, not as an own-site
   furniture launch. Highest demand and (as of 24 Jul 2026) a moderate tariff (10% Section 301 on non-upholstered wooden furniture;
   25% on upholstered wood-frame seating). Enter via (a) small décor under 10 kg/piece on Etsy / Amazon Global Selling (marketplace
   collects tax and payment; sheesham pieces under 10 kg need no CITES permit) and (b) B2B/wholesale container enquiries via the existing
   quote-lead capture (decision 0034). Own-site US checkout needs Stripe/PayPal or Razorpay-international, sales tax, carrier and legal pages.
3. **Also research in Phase 2: UAE** as the "easiest export" candidate (lowest duty/compliance, near, English, COD culture in the Gulf
   — ASSUMPTION), pending real price/competitor data we could not find.
4. **Do not launch:** EU (EUDR from 30 Dec 2026 + languages), Australia (mandatory biosecurity treatment), Canada (no evidence of pull; new
   safeguard duty on wood cabinets 31 Jul 2026), Saudi (15% duty + SABER conformity).

**Confidence:** high that India is the right first-100 market; medium that US is the right export #1 (demand is verified, winnability
depends on landed cost we could not quantify without owner unit costs); low on UAE/UK competitor price bands (no price data found).

### Why "hybrid" is the right shape
- India D2C now: fastest feedback loop for the customisation offer, reviews, UGC. Reviews and photos earned in India are reusable
  on Etsy/Amazon/Instagram for export later.
- Export upside is real but tariff-volatile: EPCH wooden-handicraft exports fell **16.6% to USD 840M in FY26** (provisional) after the 2025
  US tariff shock (IBEF, updated Jul 2026). Do not build a plan that needs the US to be cheap forever.
- Container B2B is the only way to capture the 30%-cheaper-than-market advantage on large furniture abroad; single-piece DTC freight
  eats it (see freight below).

## 4. Cross-market facts (VERIFIED unless stated)

### 4.1 Timber / species compliance
- **Sheesham = Dalbergia sissoo, CITES Appendix II.** Since CoP19 (Nov 2022) India may ship any number of sissoo articles in one
  consignment **without a CITES permit if each item is under 10 kg net timber weight**; items over 10 kg need a CITES permit or an EPCH
  "Vriksh" comparable certificate (DownToEarth 2022-11; PIB PRID 1877691). Practical effect: **beds, dining tables, wardrobes in sheesham
  need permits; small décor, trays, stools, wall shelves usually do not.** CoP20 (Nov-Dec 2025) - we found no evidence it changed this
  (not confirmed; re-check on cites.org). Mango, acacia, teak-plantation, reclaimed wood are not Dalbergia (mango/acacia not CITES-listed - ASSUMPTION from general knowledge; confirm species by lab/legal supplier docs).
  Importing countries can add their own paperwork (US FWS/APHIS for Dalbergia - confirm with a customs broker).
- **USA Lacey Act:** importer must file a plant declaration for chapter 94 wooden furniture (APHIS). Source: aphis.usda.gov/plant-imports/file-lacey-act-declaration.
- **EU:** EUDR applies to wooden furniture (HS 9403 subheadings) from **30 Dec 2026 for all operator sizes**; India is **low risk** in the
  first benchmarking list (Reg. 2025/1093) so simplified due diligence, but geolocation/traceability data for the wood is still required
  (trade.ec.europa.eu Access2Markets; tracextech.com). Commission closed further delay talk in May 2026 (secondary source; re-check).
- **UK:** UK Timber Regulation - due diligence on the first placer on the market (species, country, supplier, legality). 
- **Australia:** Illegal Logging Prohibition Rules 2024 (from 3 Mar 2025) + DAFF biosecurity: solid timber furniture needs approved treatment
  (fumigation / heat / kiln) evidenced at import.
- **ISPM-15:** wooden pallets/crates must be heat-treated/stamped; for LCL/courier use cartons + corner protection where possible.

### 4.3 Freight (FAST, ESTIMATE - vendor blogs, not quotes)
- **Sea LCL India->USA: ~USD 70-150/CBM** with 1-2 CBM minimum plus origin/destination handling; quotes of "$80/CBM" often land at
  USD 140-200/CBM all-in; transit 28-42 days (suaidglobal.com 2026; freightamigo.com). India->Europe LCL ocean USD 55-140/CBM + ~USD 80-150 handling (suaidglobal.com).
- **FCL 20ft India->US USD 2,500-4,000, 6-10 weeks door-to-door** (freightamigo.com).
- **Express courier (DHL) India->USA ~INR 918-1,118/kg (2026)**, volumetric divisor 5000 (cargocharges.com; ubtpro.in - Sep 2026). At ASSUMED INR 85-95/USD that is ~USD 10-13/kg,
  so a 10 kg décor box costs ~USD 100+ to ship: **courier only works for compact, high-value pieces.**
- Jodhpur has a dedicated rail "Furniture Express" to Mundra (launched 28 Feb 2026, 2-3 rakes/week, empty containers positioned in Jodhpur) - helps FCL/LCL export cost (indiashippingnews.com, Sep 2026). Transit time/cost not published.
- Price-advantage maths (ASSUMPTION, needs owner costs): a 30% discount on a INR 30,000 bed is ~INR 9,000 (~USD 100); LCL freight for a bed pack (~0.8-1.2 CBM) plus duty and last mile overseas will often exceed that.
  **Small, dense-value décor and B2B containers keep the advantage; single large pieces mostly do not.**

## 7. Open questions / assumptions for the owner
1. **Budget**: total INR for ads + content for first 100 orders? Any budget for marketplace fees / a test container / sample shipments?
2. **Capacity**: units per month (by category), and lead time for made-to-order (days)? Is there ready stock for small décor?
3. **Range**: which SKUs can be made under 10 kg per piece (no CITES permit)? Which timber species per product (sheesham vs mango vs acacia vs reclaimed) and can the supplier provide legal-origin documents?
4. **Export licence/IEC** (Importer Exporter Code), GST LUT/refund handling, AD code with bank, EPCH membership (Vriksh certificates)? Any existing forwarder/CHA relationship?
5. **Packaging capability**: export-grade cartons, foam/honeycomb, ISPM-15 crates, fumigation certificate? Damage rate on current domestic shipments?
6. **Cost sheet**: real ex-factory cost + margin by SKU so we can compute landed cost abroad (we used no owner cost data).
7. **Price**: what is "30% below market" measured against - Wooden Street/Pepperfry list price or their sale price? Lists are inflated 45-62% above sale price, so the true gap may be smaller.
8. **Customisation**: which parameters (size, finish, carving) and what extra lead time? (Custom pieces are hard to return - affects export risk.)
9. **Payment accounts**: is Razorpay/Cashfree KYC complete (keys are still placeholders)? Willing to apply for Razorpay International or Stripe/PayPal?
10. **Diaspora angle**: does the owner have any contacts/relatives/wholesale buyers abroad (fastest export path)?
