# United States (US) — market profile

Date 2026-09-25. Part of [market-selection.md](market-selection.md). **Verdict: highest export demand; export #1 via hybrid (marketplaces + B2B), not own-site furniture yet.**

## Demand
- Furniture market USD 180-196B (2025) at 3-6% CAGR - ESTIMATE (IMARC, Grand View). Online share 20-25%; Amazon > Walmart > Wayfair. https://www.imarcgroup.com/united-states-furniture-market
- US = **38.69%** of Indian handicraft exports (FY25, EPCH via IBEF) - VERIFIED. https://www.ibef.org/exports/handicrafts-industry-india
- Indian furniture exports FY25 USD 1.81B, USA ~USD 863M (seair - ESTIMATE; conflicts with USD 562M FY24 from same site).
- India = ~2.0% of 2025 US furniture imports (Vietnam 22.5%, China 20.6%, Mexico 20.0%); total US furniture imports USD 61.1B, -8.9% vs 2024 (Eightx, Census-based - ESTIMATE). https://eightx.co/blog/furniture-import-cost-tracker-2026
- Trend: importers diversifying away from China toward India/Vietnam (vendor blogs, ESTIMATE). Counter-signal: wooden handicraft exports fell 16.6% in FY26 to USD 840M (IBEF, provisional).
- Etsy 2026: warm, natural, handmade wood is a stated trend; sheesham décor is listed (etsy.com/market/sheesham_wood_decor). Volumes not found.

## Preferences (limited evidence)
Rustic/vintage, reclaimed and mango/acacia solid wood, statement coffee tables, consoles, bar carts, decor. Price bands: NOT verified (Etsy/Wayfair pages blocked) - Phase 2.

## Competition
Wayfair/AllModern/Joss & Main (mango wood pages exist), Amazon, Etsy Indian sellers, importers such as The Vintage Realm and Pindel Handicraft. 

## Duties / compliance (FAST)
- +10% Section 301 (India, from 24 Jul 2026); +25% Section 232 on upholstered wooden seating; cabinets/vanities 25% -> 50% on 1 Jan 2027; non-upholstered 9403 0% additional 232; upholstered 25% -> 30% 1 Jan 2027. IEEPA / 18% deal rate invalidated (SCOTUS 20 Feb 2026). Sources in market-selection.md 4.2.
- De minimis $800 suspended for all origins (June 2026): parcels pay duty + formal/informal entry.
- Lacey Act plant declaration (APHIS). CITES: sheesham items > 10 kg need permit/Vriksh certificate; < 10 kg per item no permit.
- Sales tax: marketplace facilitators (Etsy/Amazon) collect; own-site needs nexus analysis (ASSUMPTION).

## Logistics
LCL ~USD 70-150/CBM (+handling; 28-42 d), FCL 20ft USD 2,500-4,000 (6-10 wks), DHL ~INR 918-1,118/kg. Returns from the US to India are impractical -> policy: replacement/partial refund for damage, final-sale for custom.

## Payment / legal
Cards, PayPal, Apple/Google Pay, Affirm/Klarna (ASSUMPTION on mix). Razorpay International ~3% possible. Support: IST +9.5-12.5 h from US zones.
