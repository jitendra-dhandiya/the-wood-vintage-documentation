## B1. Verdict and business model
- **US B2C, DDP ("free delivery, duties included") pricing, hybrid fulfilment:** ready-stock hero SKUs pre-positioned in a US warehouse by consolidated ocean freight (DHL Global Forwarding or another forwarder), domestic ground delivery in 3-7 days; made-to-order customisation with an honest 10-12 week lead time (5-8 weeks by air at a premium); DHL Express only for samples, replacement parts and 1-2 kg gifts priced USD 250 or more.
- **Why:** DHL Express India-to-US rack rates (Zone 8: 10 kg INR 21,600; 30 kg INR 49,031; INR 1,624 per kg above 30 kg) plus about USD 70 fixed DDP fees per parcel make every furniture SKU unprofitable by air courier, even at half of rack rate. Sea freight plus a US 3PL brings delivered landed cost to roughly half.
- **Price position:** at or 5-10% below comparable US boutique prices (not "30% cheaper"; see the logistics doc, section 8). Viable SKUs at launch: carved mandir 2.5 ft (USD 599), carved mirror 60x90 (USD 279), coffee table (USD 459), side table (USD 309), console table (USD 559), bench (USD 369), bookshelf (USD 739); marginal: jaali panel, stool, personalised kitchen set; not viable: storage trunk, plant stand. All figures are model outputs on ASSUMPTION inputs; the owner's cost sheet and freight quotes replace them.

## B3. Duties, tariffs and rules (FAST, status 2026-09-25)
- **Section 301: +10% on India from 24 Jul 2026** on top of MFN (0% on non-upholstered wooden furniture HTS 9403; 3.2% wooden ornaments HTS 4420; kitchenware HTS 4419 reported 3.2-5.3%; framed mirrors about 3.9%, unverified). Section 232: upholstered wooden seating 25%; cabinets/vanities 25% -> 50% on 1 Jan 2027; **non-upholstered 9403 furniture has no additional 232** (ihfra.org 2026-07-27; ghy.com). IEEPA tariffs (incl. the 18% India deal rate) struck down 20 Feb 2026. **Get the broker to classify each SKU, especially "bookshelf" versus cabinet.**
- **De minimis suspended for all modes since 24 Jun 2026 (Federal Register 2026-12670):** every parcel needs an entry, HTS code, declared value, origin and an importer of record.
- **Lacey Act plant declaration** (genus/species, country of harvest, quantity, value) and **CITES** for sheesham items over 10 kg net timber weight (permit or Vriksh certificate); mango, acacia and reclaimed non-Dalbergia avoid CITES but still need Lacey data. **Species per SKU is the owner's decision** (below).
- **ISPM-15:** any wood packaging must be heat-treated and stamped; use cartons for DHL Express.
- **Sales tax:** marketplaces collect and remit; on our own site we need registration where we have nexus. **Inventory in a US 3PL creates physical nexus in that state**; economic nexus thresholds are USD 100k in 41 states (CA/TX/NY USD 500k) (ESTIMATE from numeral.com/taxcloud 2026). First 100 orders (about USD 45k) stay under all economic thresholds. Get a tax advisor.
- **Consumer law and other legal pages:** see [us-legal-requirements.md](us-legal-requirements.md) (FTC ship-date rule, returns disclosure, Prop 65, product safety).
