# Australia (AU) — market profile

Date 2026-09-25. Part of [market-selection.md](market-selection.md). **Verdict: not a launch candidate - duty is zero but biosecurity/treatment and distance make it heavy.**

## Demand
- Furniture market USD 18.6B (2024) -> 32.7B (2033), 5.8% CAGR (IMARC - ESTIMATE). https://www.imarcgroup.com/australia-furniture-market
- Not in the top-6 destinations for Indian handicrafts (IBEF FY25); India-origin import values not found.

## Duties / compliance
- ECTA: former 25% furniture tariff eliminated (since 29 Dec 2022). GST 10%; overseas sellers with > AUD 75k turnover must collect GST at sale for goods < AUD 1,000 (ATO).
- Illegal Logging Prohibition Rules 2024 (from 3 Mar 2025): importer due diligence + declaration. DAFF biosecurity: timber furniture needs approved treatment (fumigation/heat/kiln/etc.), evidenced. https://www.agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/import/goods/timber
- CITES > 10 kg permit for sheesham.
