# D. Product safety and labelling per product type

Prepared 2026-09-25. Sources are CPSC https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing and eCFR unless noted; all ASSUMPTION (not re-fetched this session) - confirm with a CPSC-accepted lab or attorney [LAWYER].

## D1. General
- Every consumer product needs to comply with applicable CPSC rules. Products subject to a consumer product safety rule need a General Certificate of Conformity (GCC) issued by the importer/manufacturer. Foreign seller: since you (or your IOR) import the goods, the IOR is the "importer" who must issue the certificate; cannot be a marketplace. Section 15(b): report defects/hazards to CPSC within 24 hours. Since 2025-07-08, eFiling of certificates with CBP is being phased in (ASSUMPTION - FAST; verify date and whether it applies to your HTS codes).

## D2. Children's products (Channapatna toys, kids' items) - HIGH burden
- Children's Product Certificate (CPC) issued by the importer based on tests from a CPSC-accepted third-party lab, tracking label on product and packaging (batch, date, manufacturer), ASTM F963 toy standard (incl. small parts 16 CFR 1501, lead in substrate 100 ppm, phthalates in plasticized parts, heavy metals in surface coatings, sharp points/edges), lead in paint 90 ppm (16 CFR 1303). Age grading. Choking hazard warnings (Child Safety Protection Act). Cost per toy family test panel $300-1,500 (ESTIMATE) per SKU/material combination; lac and natural dyes on Channapatna toys need lead/heavy metal testing. Recommendation: do NOT sell toys until CPC and lab reports exist; toys are a standalone project (later phase).
- Avoid cribs, bunk beds, play yards, high chairs, infant products (durable infant product rules, registration cards) - not recommended.

## D3. Lead in paint / surface coatings (16 CFR 1303) - anything painted, lacquered
- Applies to consumer products with paint/surface coating including furniture; limit 0.009% (90 ppm) lead in dried coating. General certification advisable; test the finishes (lacquer, polish, kumkum, gold leaf). Test cost $100-300 per coating/material (ESTIMATE).

## D4. Clothing storage units (STURDY Act, 16 CFR 1261)
- Applies to freestanding clothing storage units >= 27 inches high (chests, dressers, wardrobes, armoires; "storage chests" over 27 in). Needs stability testing (ASTM F2057-23 equivalent), performance requirement (tip-over with 50-lb weight, on carpet), hang tag with stability rating, permanent label, warning label and anchor kit/instructions. A General Certificate + third-party test not required for adult furniture, but certificate + tracking label needed. Storage trunks/blanket boxes not "clothing storage units" if not doors/drawers... a blanket chest with lid only isn't in scope - verify with CPSC guidance (ASSUMPTION). Bookshelves/coffee tables not in scope; add anti-tip hardware voluntarily (ASTM F3096) to reduce liability.

## D5. Upholstery flammability
- CA TB 117-2013 (cigarette smoulder) for upholstered seating filling in California; federal 16 CFR 1640 upholstered furniture rule was proposed, not final as of my knowledge. Upholstered items also go to 232 25% tariff. Recommendation: avoid upholstery/cushions initially; or ship without cushion (buyer sews). Flame retardant chemical labelling (CA SB 1019) requires "contains/no added flame retardants" statement on upholstered furniture (ASSUMPTION).

## D6. Composite wood - formaldehyde (TSCA Title VI / CARB Phase 2)
- If plywood, MDF, particleboard, or hardwood plywood with veneer core are used in furniture (drawer bottoms, back panels, mandir bases), the finished products need "TSCA Title VI Compliant" label; composite panels must be certified by a TPC (third-party certifier). The importer must have a supplier who buys certified panels; finished goods sourced from India need panel certification records. 40 CFR Part 770; EPA https://www.epa.gov/formaldehyde/formaldehyde-emission-standards-composite-wood-products (ASSUMPTION). Solid wood is exempt; laminated veneer with solid core? Prefer solid wood only. If any ply is used, label the piece with "Made with TSCA Title VI compliant composite wood" and the CARB TPC number, keep supplier's certificate. Cost of certified ply higher (Rs +10-20%).

## D7. California Prop 65
- Warning required if product exposes to listed chemicals above safe harbor: lead in paints/lacquers/brass hardware, wood dust (in listed carcinogens - wood dust generally listed; applies to exposure, mostly in occupational contexts), formaldehyde (composite wood), DEHP/phthalates (plastic). 60+ notice-and-cure lawsuits; business with < 10 employees is exempt (ASSUMPTION - Prop 65 exemption for businesses with fewer than 10 employees applies; however, Indian small manufacturers often qualify but marketplaces may still require warnings). Website: https://oehha.ca.gov/proposition-65 . New CA regulations (2018) require the warning text on the site/listing for online sales. Cheap fix: test coatings, use "safe finish" and show "no lead" test report; add conditional warning at checkout for CA shipping if not tested.

## D8. Food-contact (serving/kitchen wood: spoons, cutting boards, trays)
- FDA regulates food-contact substances (21 CFR 175-178 for coatings/adhesives). Wood is a "food-contact article" with no FDA premarket approval or facility registration required for the article; food-facility registration and Prior Notice apply to FOOD, not to non-food articles (ASSUMPTION - verify at https://www.fda.gov/food/food-ingredients-packaging). Requirements: finishes must be food-grade (mineral oil, beeswax, FDA 21 CFR 175.300 compliant coatings); avoid lacquers unless certified; heavy metals in glazes/paint; state laws (California Prop 65 lead). Label "hand-wash only; not dishwasher safe; decorative only" where finish not food-safe. Include declaration of compliance from finish supplier. If not verified, sell as "decorative, not for food contact".

## D9. Lamps and electricals
- Lamps with electrical parts need NRTL listing (UL/ETL) to be sellable on Amazon and to meet retailer standards; wiring/cords/plugs (120V US plug), CPSC lamp rules. Recommendation: do not export electrical lamps at first; sell wooden bases without electricals or "lamp shells" only. Candle holders: state candle-safe warnings.

## D10. Mirrors and glass
- Wall mirrors: no federal CPSC rule specifically; ANSI Z97.1 safety glazing applies to architectural glazing (not decor mirrors) - ASSUMPTION. Hanging hardware and weight labelling advised. Edge protection; shipping breakage insurance. Tempered/laminated backing preferable.

## D11. Warranty and labels
- Written warranties: Magnuson-Moss (see file 06). Labels: "Made in India", species, dimensions, weight, care, anti-tip (if applicable), Prop 65 (if needed), TSCA VI (if ply), tracking label (children's).
