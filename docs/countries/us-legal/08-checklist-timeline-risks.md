## K. Top-10 ways an India seller gets into trouble
1. Undeclared/false wood species on invoices and Lacey filings (goods seized; criminal exposure).
2. Selling sheesham without CITES paperwork or relying on an unverified 10 kg exemption.
3. Wrong HS code (upholstered vs non-upholstered, mandir classification) and undervaluation.
4. DAP surprises: customers refuse delivery after duty and broker fees (de minimis is gone).
5. Ignoring the FTC Mail/Internet Order Rule: no stated ship date, silent delays, no cancel option on made-to-order.
6. Selling toys/children's items without CPC and lab tests.
7. Ignoring sales-tax nexus (especially inventory in a US 3PL) and not tracking state thresholds.
8. Fake/incentivised reviews or undisclosed influencers (16 CFR 465 penalties).
9. Marketing as "rosewood" or "antique/vintage" for new goods; missing "Made in India" marking.
10. Missing tariff changes: 232/301 shifts (FAST) with prices fixed; not renewing bond/POA; missing 5-year records; SMS marketing without TCPA consent.
