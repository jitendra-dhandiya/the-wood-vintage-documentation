## F1. Documents to publish
| Doc | Content | When |
|---|---|---|
| Terms of Service / Terms of Sale | Seller identity (name, India address), governing law and venue, price in USD, made-to-order clauses, IP, limitation of liability (consumer-fair), dispute resolution/arbitration (be careful), tax and duties clause | Before first order |
| Privacy Policy | What is collected, purposes, sharing with processors, retention, rights. State laws: CCPA/CPRA (California) applies to businesses over $25M revenue or 100k+ consumers/households or 50%+ revenue from selling/sharing personal data (thresholds ASSUMPTION, adjusted for CPI); other states (VA, CO, CT, TX, OR, etc.) thresholds mostly 100k consumers (TX has no threshold except small-business exemption). At small scale most do not legally apply but marketplace and processors expect one; publish anyway. "Do Not Sell/Share" link needed if you sell/share data with ad networks and meet thresholds. Global Privacy Control signal must be honoured if applicable. COPPA: site is not directed to children under 13; do not collect their data; state so. Also India DPDP Act 2023 applies to the Indian operator [CA/LAWYER] | Before first order |
| Shipping policy | Ship-from India, processing/lead time, transit time, carriers, tracking, DDP statement: "all import duties and taxes are included; nothing to pay at delivery" (only if true), customs clearance, delays, damage claims, address/no PO boxes | Before first order |
| Returns/refund policy | Return window (Amazon-style 30 days recommended), who pays return freight (returning wooden furniture to India is uneconomic - use refund-without-return for low value or partial refund/replacement), custom items final sale where lawful, refund timing (within 7 days of receipt is a US norm; card chargeback rules 120 days), damage in transit claim windows (report within 48-72 hours with photos) | Before first order |
| Warranty | If you state a written warranty on a consumer product costing > $15, Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act requires designation (full/limited), pre-sale availability of terms (Pre-Sale Availability Rule 16 CFR 702) and plain-language terms (16 CFR 701). Optional: no written warranty, only "handmade, natural variation" statement. Avoid the phrase "lifetime" unless defined | Before first order |
| FTC Mail, Internet or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (16 CFR 435) | Must have a reasonable basis for stated shipping time; if no time is stated, 30 days is the default. If you cannot ship within the promised time, send a delay notice with a free method to cancel; after 30 more days without consent, cancel and refund promptly (within 7 business days; credit card within 1 billing cycle). Made-to-order (4-8 weeks) MUST be stated clearly at checkout and on the product page. Penalties up to ~$53,000 per violation (2025 adjusted, FAST - ASSUMPTION). Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule | Before first order (critical) |
| FTC Act section 5: truthful advertising | No unsubstantiated claims ("sustainable", "eco-friendly", "FSC" without cert, "handcrafted" if machine-made, "antique/vintage" if new - "The Wood Vintage" brand name and "vintage" usage risk: describe as "vintage-inspired"), "Made in India" honesty, species naming | Ongoing |
| Endorsement Guides + Consumer Reviews and Testimonials rule (16 CFR 465, effective 2024-10-21) | No fake/purchased reviews, review suppression, or insider reviews without disclosure; influencers/affiliates must disclose ("#ad", "gifted"); civil penalties per violation | Before running any influencer/review programme |
| CAN-SPAM | Email marketing: accurate headers, physical postal address, unsubscribe honoured within 10 business days; applies to commercial email even from abroad | Before first email campaign |
| TCPA / SMS and WhatsApp marketing | Prior express written consent for marketing texts/autodialed messages to US numbers; opt-out honoured; quiet hours; state mini-TCPA (FL, OK, MD) | Before any SMS/WhatsApp marketing to US numbers |
| ADA / WCAG | Websites of businesses serving US public are targets of demand letters. Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA (alt text, contrast, keyboard, forms labels), publish accessibility statement with contact. Overlays do not protect. Cost $1k-5k audit (ESTIMATE) | Before scaling |
| Cookie/tracking consent | US is opt-out for most; CA requires honouring opt-out and GPC; some states (CT, CO) require universal opt-out. Use consent banner with "Reject all" for US/EU visitors; also required for Meta Pixel/TikTok Pixel with sharing. Video Privacy Protection Act and wiretap ("session replay") suits in CA/PA: avoid session replay tools without consent | Before running paid ads |
| Payments | PCI-DSS SAQ-A by using hosted fields/redirect (Stripe/PayPal/Razorpay); price and checkout in USD; show final price incl. shipping/duties; display sales tax at checkout if collecting; retain chargeback evidence (tracking, delivery signature for > $500 furniture); dispute win rate improves with signature | Before first order |
| Age/child rules | Do not target children; toys marketed to children -> COPPA + CPSIA; do not collect DOB below 13 | Before first order |
| Refund timelines | Card refund within 7 business days of approval; comply with FTC rule above; state "automatic renewal" laws N/A | Before first order |
