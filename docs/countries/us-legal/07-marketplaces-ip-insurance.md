# G. Marketplaces, H. IP/brand and insurance

Prepared 2026-09-25.

## G1. Etsy
- Seller verification: identity + bank; India shops use Etsy Payments via Payoneer (VERIFIED secondary: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-in/articles/6742925359255-How-to-Accept-Payments-as-a-Seller-in-India , Etsy resumed onboarding of Indian sellers in 2026 per shipglobal.in). GSTIN and personal PAN required per secondary blogs; Indian shops can sell to international buyers only (VERIFIED secondary). Payoneer withdrawals to INR need FIRA and export purpose code [CA].
- Handmade policy: items must be made or designed by seller; production partner (workshop) must be disclosed in the shop's "About" and listing ("Made by a seller / Made by a production partner"); resellers barred. The Wood Vintage as a Jodhpur manufacturer with own carpenters is "made by seller". Source https://www.etsy.com/legal/handmade (ASSUMPTION).
- Etsy prohibited/regulated: CITES wood needs compliance with law; children's products need CPSIA certs; Etsy shows a "Made in" and expects accurate species. Fees: $0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + 3% + $0.25 payment (ASSUMPTION).

## G2. Amazon
- Amazon Global Selling (Amazon.com from India): documents: PAN, GST certificate, IEC, bank account (INR/ USD via Payoneer/Amazon Currency Converter/ Hyperwallet), government ID, business address proof, credit card, tax interview (W-8BEN-E), product compliance docs (Amazon requires CPC for kids products; UL for lamps; TSCA VI for composite wood; Prop 65). Category gating: Furniture is open; Toys & children's require compliance approval; Lamps electricals require UL. Brand Registry needs registered or pending trademark (USPTO or IP Australia etc.). Insurance: commercial general liability with $1M per occurrence once monthly sales exceed $10,000 (ASSUMPTION - verify at sellercentral). Sources: https://sell.amazon.in/sell-globally (ASSUMPTION).
- Amazon requires IOR: seller of record for Amazon Global Selling FBA imports; customs via Amazon Global Logistics or your broker.

## G3. Other channels
- Shopify (own site) with Shopify Payments not available for Indian merchants selling cross-border in some plans - use Stripe/PayPal/Razorpay. Wayfair, Faire, Houzz require US W-9/W-8, product safety and often US warehouse. Faire wholesale: retailer buyers require 1099/W-8 and cheaper duty structure. Verify each (ASSUMPTION).

## H1. Trademark
- USPTO: a foreign-domiciled applicant must be represented by a US-licensed attorney (37 CFR 2.11) (ASSUMPTION - verify at https://www.uspto.gov). Filing fee $350 per class base (2025 fee), attorney fees $500-1,500; a use-in-commerce or Section 44(e)/66(a) basis; timeline 12-18 months to registration (ESTIMATE). Classes: 20 (furniture), 21 (kitchenware), 35 (retail services), 28 (toys), 11 (lamps). Do a knockout search: "wood vintage" may be descriptive/weak; there are existing "Vintage Wood" marks - risk of refusal [LAWYER].
- Madrid Protocol from India: file international application through the Indian Trademark Registry based on Indian registration/application; designate US; costs CHF 653 base + US designation fee (~CHF 400 per class) (ASSUMPTION); US designation still examined by USPTO and may need US attorney at office-action stage. 
- Brand protection: register thewoodvintage.com and lookalike domains, social handles, keep Amazon Brand Registry once TM pending.

## H2. Insurance
- Product liability (CGL) $1M per occurrence for Amazon and most US retailers; overseas product liability from Indian insurers (e.g., export product liability policies) or via US insurers (Next, Hiscox). Premium $500-2,500/yr for small handicraft sellers (ESTIMATE). Cargo/transit insurance: 0.3-1% of value. Carrier claims: file within carrier windows (DHL/FedEx 21-60 days); keep original packaging photos; declare value.
