# United Kingdom (GB) — market profile

Date 2026-09-25. Part of [market-selection.md](market-selection.md). **Verdict: promising after CETA; not first pilot - watchlist for Q1 2027.**

## Demand
- Furniture market USD 22.8B (2025), 3.9% CAGR (IMARC - ESTIMATE); households spent ~GBP 20.4B on furniture/furnishings 2025 (Statista); online ~15% of sales; imports ~40% of market (2024) - ESTIMATE. https://www.imarcgroup.com/uk-furniture-market
- UK = **7.73%** of Indian handicraft exports FY25 (IBEF); UK among top destinations for Indian wooden handicrafts (EPCH FY25) - VERIFIED.
- Search volume and preferences: not found.

## Duties / compliance
- UK-India CETA in force 15 Jul 2026. Furniture-specific rate not verified. Origin proof/self-certification needed. https://www.business.gov.uk/campaign/alive-with-opportunity/the-uk-india-trade-deal/
- UK VAT 20%: for consignments <= GBP 135 the overseas seller must register and charge at checkout; above GBP 135 import VAT at border (gov.uk). Furniture bundles are usually above GBP 135.
- UKTR timber due diligence for first placer on the market. CITES > 10 kg.

## Logistics / payments / ops
