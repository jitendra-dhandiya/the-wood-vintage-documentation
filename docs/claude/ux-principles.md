# UX Principles

From MASTER-PROMPT §13: **high engagement without dark patterns.** Ethical behavioral design only —
progressive discovery, visual hierarchy, storytelling, social proof, personalization, comparison,
related/recently-viewed products, material/room/style-based discovery, smart recommendations, clear
CTAs, micro-interactions, scroll storytelling.

**Never:** hide essential info, fake urgency, make checkout hard to exit, deceptive buttons, fake
scarcity, fake reviews, manipulate consent.

Homepage structure reference (§12) and engagement KPIs to instrument (§14) live in
`MASTER-PROMPT.md` directly — don't implement every section blindly; validate with analytics
(§12) once instrumentation exists (Phase 7).
