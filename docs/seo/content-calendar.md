## Content production workflow (per article, 5 steps)
1. Brief from `india-seo-plan.md` section 4 + keyword row (real volume/PAA) -> 2. Draft answer-first with real photos/quotes from workshop -> 3. On-page checklist (india-seo-plan 3.2) + schema -> 4. Publish + add to sitemap + internal links from 2 existing pages + share to social with UTM -> 5. Log in the keyword sheet; review in GSC at day 14/45/90 and update.

## Refresh policy
Every article reviewed at 90 days and then every 6 months; date changes only when content changes (Google guidance: don't fake freshness). Seasonal pages (Diwali, wedding) keep their URL, updated each year before the season.

## Content-quality rules (non-negotiable)
Own photos; named author; real numbers with the date; no copying competitor text; no AI-generated filler without expert review; no invented sources; disclose gifted/sponsored items; medical/safety claims (children's toys) verified.
