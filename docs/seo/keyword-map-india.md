# Keyword universe and keyword map — India

Date: 2026-09-25. Companion to [india-seo-plan.md](india-seo-plan.md). Tags: VERIFIED / ESTIMATE / ASSUMPTION.

## 0. Read this first — what this document is and is not

- **No search volumes are stated.** Google Keyword Planner, Semrush, Ahrefs and Google Trends were not accessible to this research pass (WebSearch returned no volume data - checked 2026-09-25; `countries/market-selection.md` section 8 records the same gap). Every number-like column below is a **relative tier** (my judgement, ESTIMATE), not data.
- Tiers were built from: (a) the language competitors actually use in titles/H1s (VERIFIED for Wooden Street, Woodsala, Timber Guy below), (b) buying-journey logic, (c) what the owner can make and ship.
- **Owner: replace tiers with real numbers using the free method in section 6**, then re-sort. Expect the order of a few rows to change; the page mapping should hold.
- A keyword is only worth a page **if we can make/sell the thing today** (owner to confirm range; the production catalogue is 2 crafts items, locally 46). Rows marked `RANGE?` need a real product first — do not create a landing page for furniture we cannot photograph.

### Tier definitions
- **Intent**: T = transactional (ready to buy/quote), C = commercial investigation (comparing), I = informational, N = navigational/brand.
- **Funnel**: BOFU (buy/quote), MOFU (compare/choose), TOFU (learn/inspire).
- **Relative demand tier (D)**: H / M / L — how many people plausibly type it, relative to the others in this sheet (ESTIMATE; verify).
- **Difficulty tier (K)**: **Hard** = owned by Wooden Street/Pepperfry/Urban Ladder/IKEA/Amazon/Flipkart + big publishers (a new domain will not rank in year 1); **Med** = competitive but a focused page with strong content/links could reach page 1-2 in 6-12 months; **Win** = long-tail/custom/provenance/guide terms where a small specialist can realistically rank in 3-9 months (ESTIMATE, not measured).
- **Priority**: P0 = build before/at launch; P1 = first 60 days; P2 = days 60-120; P3 = later/only if data supports.
- **Status**: `EXIST` = route exists today (`/in/...`), `NEW` = page to be created later (development on hold), `BLOG` = article (see content calendar / 30 briefs in india-seo-plan.md).

### Where a small brand can realistically win (reasoning)
Competitor pages (VERIFIED 2026-09-25): Wooden Street's "Buy Sheesham Wood Bed at upto 50% Off (Latest Designs) | Wooden Street" page has ~1,200 words, filters for finish/size/storage, **no FAQ section**, and leads with discount language (https://www.woodenstreet.com/sheesham-wood-beds). Woodsala (Jodhpur, "Manufacturing Since 2001", "1 Year Warranty", 98% safe delivery, "from 1225 reviews") already sells the "quote in 24 hours / complete customisation" message with title "Online Furniture Store | Buy Wooden Furniture Online" (https://www.woodsala.com/). The Timber Guy (Sunrise International, Jodhpur, online since 2004) ranks its `sheesham-wood-furniture` collection. So head terms are taken; **custom/made-to-order + honest buying guides + real workshop provenance + city-specific delivery/lead-time answers** are the gaps. Weakness of the incumbents: generic copy, "up to 50% off" pricing noise (Wooden Street sale price vs MRP = 45-62% "off", VERIFIED in `countries/in.md`), thin guidance on "is this really sheesham", "how long does made-to-order take", "will it fit my room".

### D. City and pincode modifiers — use carefully (doorway risk)
| Keyword | Int | D | K | Target | Status | Pri |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| furniture in Jaipur / Delhi / Mumbai / Bangalore / Hyderabad / Pune (local-pack style "furniture shop near me") | T local | H | **Hard** (local pack + local stores) | Not targetable by an online-only D2C brand as head terms | - | skip |
| solid wood furniture delivery in Delhi NCR / Mumbai / Bangalore / Jaipur / Chandigarh | T | M | Win-Med | ONE page `/in/delivery` ("Where we deliver, lead times, ODA charges, installation") with a real table by metro + section per metro only where we have real facts (delivery days, past orders, installation partner) | NEW | P1 |
| jodhpur furniture delivery to [city] | T | L | Win | same `/in/delivery` (+ blog "shipping furniture from Jodhpur to Bangalore: what to expect") | NEW + BLOG | P2 |
| furniture showroom in Jodhpur / near me | T local | M | Med | GBP + `made-in-jodhpur` (only if visitable) | NEW | P2 |

**City page policy (doorway guard):** no `/in/furniture-in-jaipur` template pages. A city page is allowed only if it contains information that is *true and different per city*: real delivery days (from carrier data), real ODA/freight cost bands, installation availability, 2+ real delivered orders with photos/quotes, local climate note (e.g., humidity/monsoon wood care in Mumbai vs dry Jaipur). Start with the single `/in/delivery` page; graduate to city pages when we have >= 3 real deliveries in that city. Otherwise Google treats them as doorway pages (Google spam policies).

## 2. Priority summary (what to build first, in order)

1. **P0 (launch set)**: `custom-furniture`, `how-customisation-works`, `made-in-jodhpur`, product pages for live SKUs (Krishna panel, stacking rings), collection `heritage-living` optimisation, 6 anchor guides (sheesham vs mango, sheesham vs teak, is sheesham good, solid vs engineered wood, identify real sheesham, wood quality checklist), brand queries, home.
2. **P1 (days 1-60)**: category pages for whatever furniture is really in range (beds, tables/consoles), `/in/delivery`, gifting + Diwali/housewarming, care + finish guides, spice box/cutting board, GBP.
3. **P2-P3**: comparisons, city pages (only with facts), B2B/corporate, Hindi, outdoor, wardrobes, temples.

## 3. Cannibalisation guard
One primary keyword per URL; one URL per intent. Examples: "sheesham wood bed price" -> `category/beds` (transactional); "how much does a sheesham bed cost" -> blog (informational) that links to it; "sheesham vs mango wood" -> only the blog, not `/material/*`.

## 4. Competitor language to learn from (VERIFIED titles/H2s, 2026-09-25)
- Wooden Street H2s: "Price List", "Choose from Different ... Designs", "Explore ... in Different Sizes", "Things to Consider while Buying", "Advantages of Buying ... Online", "Why Buy ... from Wooden Street?" - copy the *structure* (buying-guide sections on a category page), not the words; add the missing FAQ and custom-size options.
- Woodsala: "Get a quote in 24 hours by sharing your product's design, material, and finish" - our quote flow (0034, "lead_response_promise: within 1 business day" already in site settings) must be at least as fast and more visible.
- Timber Guy: "Solid Sheesham wood furniture, selling online since 2004" - do not claim "since" anything unless true; our honest angle is "made by our own carpenter team in Jodhpur".

## 5. Keyword-difficulty reality check
A new domain (Sept 2026) with ~0 backlinks will not rank in year 1 for A-section head terms (`sheesham wood bed price`, `wooden dining table`). Target the **Win** column first; use head-term pages as conversion pages for paid/social/marketplace traffic and as long-term SEO assets.

## 6. How the owner pulls real numbers (free) + template
