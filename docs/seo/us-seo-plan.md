## 0. Bottom line
1. **For the first 100 orders own-site SEO earns almost nothing:** the whole site is `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` until go-live (F3), product pages fail for crawlers today (F1/F2), and a new domain needs 9-18 months for meaningful non-brand traffic (ESTIMATE, [us-uae-seo-plan.md](us-uae-seo-plan.md) section 7). **The US search surface for our products is Amazon, Etsy, Pinterest and Google Shopping free listings first; own-site SEO is a long-term asset that must be started correctly, not a source of the first 100 orders.**
2. **Do now (cheap, durable):** fix indexability preconditions, write the guides that answer US questions ("what size mandir", "solid wood vs laminate", "mango vs sheesham"), publish the custom-furniture page, get Merchant Center free listings live, and build a handful of real links.
3. **Marketplace SEO first:** listing copy, images and reviews decide rank on Amazon and Etsy ([../marketing/social/11-us-diaspora-and-mainstream-playbook.md](../marketing/social/11-us-diaspora-and-mainstream-playbook.md) section 8).
4. **Geo-lock and noindex (decision 0036, F3):** with a US-first launch the lock aligns with Googlebot's mostly US IPs, but several decisions are new (section 6).
5. **Wood naming decides trust and ranking:** "solid mango wood" is a mainstream US term; "sheesham" is niche; "Indian rosewood" is recognised; **"rosewood" alone maps to protected species**.

## 2. US keyword map
### 2.1 Language rules for the US
US English spelling and units (color, finish, inches, lb; cm second). **Wood naming:** lead with "solid mango wood" for mango pieces; use "sheesham (Indian rosewood, *Dalbergia sissoo*)" only for real sheesham and only with the species stated; never "rosewood" alone; never "teak" for mango/sheesham. "Mandir", "pooja/puja unit" and "home temple" are all used; include all three. "Jharokha" and "jaali" need a plain-English gloss ("carved window-frame mirror", "carved lattice panel") because mainstream buyers do not know them.

## 3. Marketplace SEO in short
- **Amazon:** relevance (title, bullets, backend terms), conversion (images, price, reviews, delivery promise), sales velocity (launch coupon, PPC). New listings need reviews: compliant methods only ([../marketing/social/11-us-diaspora-and-mainstream-playbook.md](../marketing/social/11-us-diaspora-and-mainstream-playbook.md) section 9). Brand Registry (needs a trademark filing) unlocks A+ content, Brand Analytics and Vine.
- **Etsy:** relevance (title, 13 tags, attributes, categories), listing quality (photos, video, clicks and conversion), shop quality (fast replies, on-time shipping, reviews). Set honest processing times: made to order 10-12 weeks; Etsy shoppers see it. Ranking rules summary (marmalead.com, 2026, ESTIMATE).
- **Pinterest:** keyword-rich pin titles, boards, fresh pins weekly, claim the site.
- Reuse assets across all three; **price parity** across channels.

## 6. Geo-lock (decision 0036), crawler exemption and noindex removal for a US launch
**Facts (VERIFIED in the technical checklist, 2026-09-25):** the whole site is `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` (nginx, lines 51-52 of `operations/server/nginx-site.conf`); product pages return "Product Not Found" to crawlers today (F1) and the sitemap has no product URLs (F2); the geo-lock redirects humans by IP and exempts crawlers by User-Agent only (spoofable; no reverse-DNS check); root `/` redirects 307 to `/in`.
| # | Implication for the US launch | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | **noindex removal gates:** all seven gates in the checklist (section 2.1) must hold **for `/us`**: F1/F2 fixed, real business data, a payment path or an honest quote-only mode, **>= 12-15 real products with 3+ real photos**, no seeded content, canonicals, Search Console verified, and social already running. We have 11 SKUs and 6 combos: acceptable only when each has 3+ real photos | Use the **staged release**: index `/us` home, custom-furniture, guides and category pages first; product pages when each meets the photo/data floor |
| G2 | **Googlebot crawls mostly from US IPs**, which fits `/us` as the served market; it is the `/in` pages that a US-IP crawler could be bounced from (the exemption covers this by UA) | Verify crawlers by Google's published IP ranges/reverse-DNS (W-07) so the exemption cannot be spoofed; add `googleother` and `storebot-google` to the allow list if the regex lacks them |
| G3 | **Who is turned away:** with `/us` the only served market, diaspora visitors in Canada, the UK and Australia (a large part of the Indian diaspora) see "Not available". They are outside our first market by decision but are the same buyers | Add the notify-me capture on `/not-available` (0036 follow-up) so they are not lost; consider Canada as a later market |
| G4 | **Hard 307 for organic landing pages** is the asymmetry Google discourages; with `/us` first, a **soft banner** ("You appear to be in X; view the X store") for humans on organic landings and a hard lock only at checkout is safer | Needs the owner's OK (0036 was a client requirement) |
| G5 | **`/us` preview state:** decision 0031's sample prices and blocked non-INR payments make the current `/us` unsafe to index | Do not remove noindex before T1 and Gate C |
| G6 | **Root `/` -> `/in`:** wrong once India is out of scope; a US visitor is redirected by IP, but Googlebot and shared links land on `/in` | Point the default market to `/us`; x-default `/us` |
| G7 | **`/not-available`** returns HTTP 200 + noindex (fine); keep it out of the sitemap | keep |
| G8 | **Owner decision needed:** what Indian visitors see (they are not customers): `/in` as a content/B2B quote page, or not-available | see the owner decisions in [../marketing/master-growth-plan.md](../marketing/master-growth-plan.md) |
