# USA and UAE competitor analysis (India-origin wooden furniture and décor)

Date: 2026-09-25. Phase-1 conclusion applied: **first 100 orders = India domestic**; **export pilot = USA hybrid** (small décor under 10 kg per item via Etsy/Amazon Global,
plus B2B/container enquiries); **secondary research market = UAE**. See [../countries/market-selection.md](../countries/market-selection.md), [../countries/us.md](../countries/us.md),
[../countries/uae.md](../countries/uae.md). Prices in USD / AED as listed, not converted (owner to fix an FX rate - ASSUMPTION: INR 88 per USD and INR 24 per AED, unverified).
Tags: VERIFIED (read live 2026-09-25), ESTIMATE (snippet/blog), ASSUMPTION.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **US:** the market is huge, but **online price data for Etsy, Amazon and Wayfair could not be fetched** (Etsy 403, Amazon 503, Wayfair 429). What we could verify: India-made mango-wood décor is stocked by a mainstream US retailer (World Market mango wood footed tray from India, **USD 24.99 medium / 34.99 large**), US-listed "Handmade in India" trays are best sellers on Amazon (MyGift, AuldHome listings seen; prices not read), and the one US-facing specialist we could crawl (The Vintage Realm) prices reclaimed-wood furniture at **dining tables ~USD 1,467, console tables ~USD 517, coffee tables ~USD 492 median** with 23% flat compare-at. World Market mango-wood coffee tables list at **USD 599.99-1,199.99** (sale prices not captured).
2. **The US door for a Jodhpur maker is small décor (under 10 kg per item, no CITES permit) and B2B**, not single furniture pieces by courier. Express freight from India (~INR 918-1,118/kg per us.md) can exceed the item's retail price for trays/boards (unit-economics-model.md section 6).
3. **UAE:** the most useful finding is that **UAE importers already sell Rajasthan-origin solid wood at scale**: in The Antiques Furniture's catalogue **213 of 242 product descriptions mention India, 102 mention Rajasthan/Jodhpur, 74 mention sheesham**; Art & Craft Furniture lists mango-wood dining tables at **AED 1,537-3,000** with **free UAE delivery, COD on all orders and Tabby/Tamara instalments**. These stores are both **competitors and prospective B2B buyers** (a Jodhpur maker can be their supplier).
4. **UAE consumers are anchored low at the top of the funnel** by IKEA (LACK coffee table AED 89; veneer/wood-look pieces AED 399-1,299) and Danube (engineered coffee tables AED 125-305 at 31-56% off), so the story that works is *solid wood, handmade, customised* versus *engineered, flat-pack*.
5. **Recommendation:** treat US and UAE as research + B2B pilots after India order ~30-50; do not build own-site checkout for either yet. Details and what to ask the owner are in sections 6-7.

**Confidence:** medium for UAE prices (crawled feeds), low-medium for the US (thin because of blocked marketplaces), low for social/ads (not collected).

### US duty and shipping reminder (FAST, from us.md)
Section 301 +10% on India (from 24 Jul 2026), de minimis suspended, Section 232 on upholstered wooden seating and (from 1 Jan 2027) cabinets; CITES/Vriksh permit for sheesham items over 10 kg; Lacey Act declaration; LCL ~USD 70-150/CBM. These make **small under-10 kg décor and B2B containers the only sensible first US products.**

### UAE buyer behaviour seen on sites (VERIFIED on Art & Craft only; others ASSUMPTION)
COD on all orders, **Tabby/Tamara** instalments, WhatsApp as a main contact channel, free delivery across UAE, free installation/assembly on big-box sites. So an India-origin brand selling to UAE consumers must offer COD or instalments and WhatsApp - both inconsistent with the current India-only checkout (decision 0027).

## 6. Where an India maker can win first
- **US:** small décor under 10 kg where landed cost is competitive (trays, boards, spice boxes, wall panels) - and only as bundles/B2B. Do **not** promise single-piece express delivery to consumers.
- **UAE:** supply-to-retailer (B2B) to importers such as Art & Craft, The Antiques Furniture, Lucky Furniture: offer customisation and price transparency; then test an own consumer store later.

## 7. Owner must replace / confirm
1. Whether you want to be a supplier to importers (B2B) or sell to consumers abroad.
2. FX rates (INR per USD and per AED), and your export costing.
3. Whether you already export or hold IEC/GST/LUT registrations.
4. Sample photo set and MOQ/price for a 20-30 piece trial shipment.
