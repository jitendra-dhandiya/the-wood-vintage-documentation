# US B2C price benchmark: India-origin solid-wood furniture, mandirs and décor (retail to US consumers)

Date: 2026-09-25. Scope: what US consumers pay today for equivalent pieces (sheesham, mango, teak, reclaimed; carved; mandir/pooja units) and what shipping/returns look like. Used to set US prices in [../marketing/export-b2c-logistics-and-landed-cost.md](../marketing/export-b2c-logistics-and-landed-cost.md). Companion: [us-b2c-competitor-analysis.md](us-b2c-competitor-analysis.md). Earlier partial view: [us-uae-competitor-analysis.md](us-uae-competitor-analysis.md).

Tags: **VERIFIED** = read on the cited page or product feed on 2026-09-25; **ESTIMATE** = search-result snippet, blog or third-party listing not opened by us; **ASSUMPTION** = ours. Prices are USD as listed (sale price where two prices show), not converted. **Prices change; re-check the week we set launch prices (FAST).**

**Reading:** the US mandir market is served by **US-stocked sellers with free ground shipping at USD 275-1,800** and a few India-fulfilled sellers with 3-5 day claims. Most cheap units are laminate or engineered ("natural teak laminate", "white gloss"). Solid carved sheesham, mango or teak at USD 500-900 is a gap between the mango-wood units at USD 410-570 and the USD 1,700+ US-made custom class. Demand context: nearly eight in ten Asian-American Hindu households have a shrine at home and 95% of Indian-American Hindus celebrate Diwali (Pew via search summary, ESTIMATE, https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2012/07/19/asian-americans-a-mosaic-of-faiths-overview/).

## 7. Customer pain points seen (what buyers complain about)

Review evidence was thin: Trustpilot pages for Timbergirl and World Interiors show 0 reviews; Mandir For Home has 17 reviews at 4.3/5, 88% five-star, none in the last 12 months (all 2021 or Oct-Nov 2024); The Mandir Store and Urli Utsav have no Trustpilot page (404). Themes from sources we could open (VERIFIED or ESTIMATE as tagged):
- **Delays and missed dates, poor customer service, damaged or defective pieces (swollen wood, broken parts) with no resolution** across Indian furniture retailers on Trustpilot (Wooden Street review pages, ESTIMATE via search summary) and for India-shipped goods; Mandir For Home has a "little delay in delivery" review and one "very bad quality" review (VERIFIED).
- **Damage on arrival on Etsy mandirs:** "broken mirror inside", "dented upper panel on the rear edge", damaged small mandirs replaced free by the shop, and a DHL duty-fee delay (Etsy listing review snippets, ESTIMATE).
- **Tariff surprises:** duty and courier collection fees on non-US shipments; Etsy's answer is mandatory DDP (2026-07-09). Urli Utsav markets "no customs charge, no brokerage fee" as its main selling point (VERIFIED).
- **Wood authenticity:** "sheesham" vs "sheesham veneer / engineered" and laminate mandirs sold as "teak finish" (Mandir Store lists "Natural Teak Laminate"); buyers cannot verify species (ESTIMATE from listing wording; no review text read).
- **Furniture return costs and "final sale" on customisation** (Mandir For Home policy VERIFIED).

## 8. Price positioning for The Wood Vintage in the US (input to positioning-and-usp.md)
- **Do not price against Wayfair commodity sheesham (USD 217-337).** Our coffee table needs about USD 432 for 25% margin; we lose money below about USD 320.
- **Sit at "boutique parity or 5-10% under": coffee table USD 459, side table USD 309, console USD 559, bench USD 369, bookshelf USD 739, mirror USD 279, mandir 2.5 ft USD 599** (see table 4 in the logistics doc), justified by carving, customisation and named wood, not by price.
- The US price band reference for "premium boutique" is Timbergirl and Vintage Realm (USD 400-650 for tables and consoles); "design-led" is World Interiors and West Elm (USD 800-1,400 for a coffee table).
- **Compare-at prices in the US are also inflated** (Timbergirl average compare-at gap about 20-30%; Vintage Realm's feed shows 55-80% between the lowest variant price and highest compare-at, ESTIMATE because variant sizes differ). Do not show a compare-at price.
