## Real finding: three of the five names are one company

**Sunrise International, The Timber Guy, and Sunrise Art & Exports appear to be the same
Jodhpur-based manufacturer/exporter operating multiple storefronts/brands**, not three independent
competitors as the master prompt's list might suggest at a glance. Worth knowing before treating
them as three separate data points in any competitive analysis.

## Directory

| Name | URL | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Woodsala | [woodsala.com](https://www.woodsala.com/) | Jodhpur, Rajasthan | Online wooden furniture retailer — beds, sofas, dining sets, storage; teak/solid wood; pan-India delivery, free installation, lifetime warranty claimed; customization offered. |
