## 1. Executive summary
1. **The competitor set has three layers:** (a) mass importers on Wayfair/Amazon at USD 217-340 for a sheesham coffee table; (b) **US-stocked boutique importers** (Timbergirl, Vintage Realm, World Interiors, Urli Utsav, The Mandir Store) selling the same Indian-made wood at USD 400-1,400, shipping from Texas, California, Idaho/Nevada; (c) **India-fulfilled specialists** (Mandir For Home, Ananta Artifacts, Etsy India shops) who promise 3-5 day delivery and free shipping and rely on DHL/UPS.
2. **The direct threat for our hero SKU (mandir) is the US-stocked layer with free ground shipping at USD 275-1,800**, mostly laminate or engineered. Our differentiation must be solid carved wood, named species, custom sizing and a verifiable Jodhpur workshop.
3. **Weak spots we can attack honestly:** thin customer-review histories (Timbergirl 0, World Interiors 0, Mandir For Home 17 reviews with none in the last year), strict no-return custom policies, vague wood claims ("teak laminate"), paid freight on furniture (World Interiors USD 99-199), and slow or unclear delivery dates.
4. **What we cannot beat:** Wayfair's price on commodity sheesham and Amazon's delivery speed. We should not try; we should stay above them with a story.
5. **Competitor economics confirm our model:** World Interiors' USD 99 economy freight and World Market's USD 30-200 furniture surcharge match our USD 105 modelled last mile for a coffee table.

## 3. Patterns to copy and avoid
- **Copy:** the "duties included, free delivery, we replace it if damaged" message (Urli Utsav); phone support hours; a 48-hour photo-claim window; live-edge and "named wood" collections (World Interiors); tiered prices that step 20-30% between sizes.
- **Avoid:** fake compare-at prices (Timbergirl, Vintage Realm), laminate sold as "teak finish", unevidenced delivery claims ("3 to 5 days" from India), and vague return policies for custom furniture.
- **Timing:** competitors already run festive assortments (Urli Utsav Diwali/gifting; Mandir Store). Diwali is 8 Nov 2026; sea-freight stock cannot arrive in time, so our first stock targets Jan-Mar 2027 occasions (Makar Sankranti/Pongal about 14 Jan 2027 (verify the date), Ugadi/Gudi Padwa and housewarming/wedding season) and Diwali 2027.

## 4. Review pain points (summary; details in the benchmark, section 7)
Delays and no updates, damage on arrival with poor after-sales, customs surprises, wood authenticity doubts, high return-shipping cost. Evidence is thin because most US boutique competitors have few or no public reviews; this is itself an opportunity: **collect verified reviews with photos from the first 30 orders and publish them.**

## 6. Where we can win (summary)
1. **Solid carved mandirs and pooja units at USD 500-900** between laminate units and US-made custom.
2. **Custom-to-your-room sizes** (most US laminate mandirs are fixed sizes; Mandir For Home does custom but with final-sale terms).
3. **Provenance and proof:** workshop video, named wood (species and origin), packing video, repair/replacement promise.
4. **Honest dates:** in-stock 3-7 days; made-to-order 10-12 weeks (or 5-8 by air at a premium).
5. **Bundles:** mandir plus pooja stool/bajot, or coffee table plus side tables (raises order value and spreads last-mile cost).
