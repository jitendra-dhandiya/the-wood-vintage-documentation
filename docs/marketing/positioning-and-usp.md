## 1. Executive summary

1. **Recommended position: "value-premium made-to-order".** Not "cheapest" (Flipkart factory sellers sell sheesham at 35-55% of brand-tier prices and will always be lower) and not "premium" (we have no brand yet). Price about **25-35% below Wooden Street / Urban Ladder selling prices and at or 5-20% below Woodsala for the same spec**, and be the one that is **honest about wood, price and delivery dates**.
2. **Five USPs that competitor reviews and policies show are open:** (1) made to your room with a WhatsApp design consult; (2) one honest price (no fake MRP) explained by factory-direct; (3) a delivery date we keep, with visible workshop progress; (4) wood named and proven; (5) repair-first 12-month workshop warranty. Each needs proof we do not yet have (section 3).
3. **Do not claim** teak for sheesham/mango, "lifetime warranty" you will not honour, "30% off" against an inflated MRP, invented reviews, or the fictional artisans/testimonials still in the demo seed (decision 0028) - publishing them would be a false claim.
4. **First-win white space:** light-to-medium heritage pieces with real customisation - **mandir units, jaali/carved panels, carved mirrors, coffee + side tables, bookshelves, custom desks** - for **first-home/housewarming buyers and wedding/festival gifters**, priced INR 4,999-17,999; kitchen-wood sets at INR 999-3,499 as low-risk gateways. **Avoid first:** wardrobes, beds, upholstered sofas, jhulas, anything with large glass, and sub-INR 1,000 décor.
5. **The price story only works if the margin holds**: at these prices coffee tables, jaali panels, dining tables and mandirs are workable, but beds/TV units/bedside tables are thin unless timber costs about INR 900/cft or CAC is organic (unit-economics-model.md).

**Confidence:** medium-high that the gaps are real (repeated across sources); medium that they translate into orders; low on any claim needing owner proof that does not exist yet.

## 6. What NOT to claim (legal and honesty)

1. **Species:** never "teak" for sheesham/mango/acacia; never "solid wood" for engineered/plywood parts; state veneers and boards. (Timber Guy/Woodsala/Wooden Street all list several species - be more precise than they are.)
2. **Warranty:** no "lifetime" unless you will pay for repairs for life; state term and exclusions.
3. **Discounts:** no compare-at price that was never charged; no "up to 70% off"; no "MRP" that is not a real reference price. Whether any compare-at is lawful is a **lawyer question** (consumer protection rules on misleading advertising) - we do not assert the law.
4. **Reviews and social proof:** none invented, bought or copied. The demo seed (decision 0028) contains **fictional testimonials, fictional artisans (8 names), sample blog posts and generated placeholder photos** - all must be removed or replaced before real traffic.
5. **Origin/craft claims:** "handcrafted", "hand-carved" only where true (machine CNC carving is common; say "CNC-cut, hand-finished" if it is); "made in Jodhpur" only for what is made there; GI-tag or certification words only with certificates.
6. **Kiln-dried / termite-proof / chemical-free:** only with process proof (Timber Guy's own page mentions copper-chrome-arsenate treatment: if you use chemicals, do not claim "chemical-free").
7. **Customer counts, awards, press:** none until real.
8. **Delivery dates:** do not promise 7-day delivery for made-to-order work.
9. **Export/CITES:** do not claim "CITES certified" - permits are per shipment (market-selection.md).

### 12.3 Pricing psychology in USD and "why we are not cheaper than Wayfair"
- **Do not compete with Wayfair commodity sheesham (USD 217-337 for a coffee table).** Our model needs about USD 432 for a 25% margin and loses money below about USD 320 (logistics doc). Being cheaper is neither possible nor good for the brand.
- **Position at boutique parity, 5-10% under comparable pieces:** coffee table USD 459, side table USD 309, console USD 559, bench USD 369, bookshelf USD 739, carved mirror USD 279, mandir 2.5 ft USD 599 (model outputs; owner's costs replace them). Anchors: Timbergirl and Vintage Realm USD 400-650 for tables and consoles, World Interiors USD 800-1,400 for a coffee table, mandir units USD 275-570 (mango/laminate) up to USD 1,700-3,300 (US-made custom).
- **Whole-dollar prices** (USD 459, 599) match the craft tier used by Urli Utsav (395, 410, 570), The Mandir Store (399, 699, 1,799) and World Interiors (824, 662); ".99" is the mass-market signal (Timbergirl, World Market) (VERIFIED pattern in the feeds, our reading).
- **One all-in number:** "Delivered to your door, duties included" (the norm at Urli Utsav, Mandir Store and Mandir For Home; World Interiors charges USD 99-199 extra). Show the delivery date next to the price.
- **Anchors and bundles:** show the bundle (living-room set, pooja corner) with a saving of about 8% versus the sum of parts; do not show a fake compare-at price (US competitors do; we do not).
- **The pitch when a customer says "Wayfair is cheaper":** "Wayfair's is a container-shipped, factory-finish table. Ours is made in our Jodhpur workshop from named solid wood in the size you choose, and if it arrives damaged we fix it. If you want the cheapest table, buy theirs; if you want this one, here is what makes it different."
- **Price-value proof:** wood species and weight, joinery (mortise-tenon, no particle board), finish coats, and a 60-second workshop video per hero SKU.
