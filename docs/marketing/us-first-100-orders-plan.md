## The plan in fifteen lines
1. **Market and model:** USA, B2C, hybrid: hero SKUs come by **LCL sea freight to a US 3PL** and ship by ground in 3-7 days; custom pieces are **made to order with an honest 10-12 week promise** (5-8 weeks by air at a premium); DHL Express only for samples and parts. "Duties included, free delivery" prices.
2. **Position:** solid carved wood from a named Jodhpur workshop, sized to your room, honest dates; **parity to about 8% below boutique comps, never "30% cheaper"**.
3. **Launch range:** 11 SKUs in the first container (about 11.2 CBM, 118 units, about USD 42,000 retail): carved mandir 2.5 ft USD 599, compact mandir 2 ft USD 449, carved mirror USD 279, jaali panel USD 219, coffee table USD 459, side table USD 309, console USD 559, bench USD 369, bookshelf USD 739, pooja stool USD 189, kitchen gift set USD 109. **Container 1 is all mango/acacia** (no sheesham, no CITES) until the CITES answer is in writing.
4. **Six combos:** Pooja corner set USD 729, Living-room accent trio USD 959, Entryway set USD 1,099, Gift pair USD 299, Compact pooja starter USD 589, Side-table pair USD 569 (about 8% under the parts, above their floors).
5. **Coupons:** eight own-site/quote codes (FIRSTUS 5% cap USD 40, referral USD 40 give/get, closing, nudge, creator, trade, SPRING27) plus Amazon coupons and Etsy sales; Diwali 2026 is missed, Diwali 2027 (29 Oct) is the first festival served.
6. **Audience:** primary Indian-American households buying mandirs and pooja pieces; secondary design-conscious mainstream buyers (Pinterest/Etsy/Amazon); tertiary gifting, stagers/designers/Airbnb hosts, wellness studios.
7. **Channels:** Amazon.com FBA for stocked heroes, Etsy for custom and photography-led pieces, Instagram/Pinterest/Facebook/WhatsApp community as the traffic engine, the own site as brand hub and quote form (checkout after about order 50).
8. **Budget (recommended Base):** about USD 25,700 before per-order shipping (goods, freight and duty about USD 10,500; paid media USD 5,850; creators, content, samples, tools USD 4,900; legal/insurance/trademark USD 4,400); **peak cash about USD 18,800 in Feb 2027; commit USD 20,000**. Lean: USD 13,800 and peak about USD 8,900. Growth: not recommended (about USD 37,250, peak about USD 32,800).
9. **Timeline:** stock lands about **18 Jan 2027**; **100th order in the week of 29 Mar 2027** (range 1 Mar to Aug 2027). The binding constraint is sea-freight arrival, not ad spend.
10. **Christmas 2026 air-freight pilot: evaluated, not recommended** ([07](us-first-100/07-budget-channel-mix-and-timeline.md) section 6).
11. **Unit economics of the first 100:** average order USD 488, contribution before acquisition USD 173, acquisition and set-up USD 152 an order: about break-even on contribution, cash-negative by design.
12. **Rules that decide it:** break-even cost per order USD 173, kill line USD 147, scale target USD 86; book container 2 at order 25.
13. **Legal must-haves before the container leaves:** HTS sheet, Lacey data, CITES stance, importer of record + bond + ISF + broker, Made in India marks, ISPM-15, invoices, lead test, insurance; before selling: policies including the **FTC ship-date rule**, sales-tax adviser, TCPA/CAN-SPAM, marketplace accounts.
14. **Customer service:** two IST windows (19:00-23:00 and 07:30-11:30) = US morning and US evening; 15-minute first reply in-window.
15. **Biggest unknowns:** freight and last-mile quotes, the owner's real US network, the cost sheet, Etsy ships-from-US rules, Amazon fees.
