Turns USP 3 of [../positioning-and-usp.md](../positioning-and-usp.md) section 12 ("custom to your room") into a menu, a process and written terms. **Every adder, percentage, timeline and policy term is an ASSUMPTION or OWNER-SET draft; the policy text is not legal advice and needs a US attorney's read** ([02](02-launch-readiness-gate.md) B1). India draft for reference: [../first-100/08-customisation-offer.md](../first-100/08-customisation-offer.md).

## 1. Why it is the moat
US competitors sell fixed sizes (most laminate mandirs) or offer custom under strict "final sale" terms with delivery claims they do not evidence (Mandir For Home: custom not returnable, "3-5 days" claimed from India). A visible menu with honest adders, a dated timeline and a repair-first warranty is the opening ([../../competitor-research/us-b2c-competitor-analysis.md](../../competitor-research/us-b2c-competitor-analysis.md) section 6). Made-to-order also **needs no inventory cash** and rides the next scheduled container.

## 3. The process: brief, design, quote, advance, build, ship, delivery

| Step | What happens | Who / where | Time (working days unless stated) | What the customer sees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Brief | Customer sends photos of the space, wall width/height, style, wood/finish, budget | Own-site quote form (decision 0034), WhatsApp, Etsy "Request a custom order", Amazon message (for made-to-order Amazon listings) | Day 0 | Confirmation and the date we reply |
| 2 First reply | Clarifying questions; check the piece is inside our range | Sales desk, US-evening/IST-morning window ([09](09-roadmap-and-operations.md)) | **within 15 min in-window; 24 h maximum** | Named contact |
| 3 Design | Dimensioned drawing in inches with a wall-fit overlay; carving sketch; finish swatch offer | Owner/designer | 2-3 days | Drawing PDF and photos of a similar finished piece |
| 4 Quote | All-in price (delivered, duties included), **dated timeline**, deposit, terms | Sales desk | **quote within 48 hours of the brief** | Written quote valid 7 days |
| 5 Advance | **40% deposit** on approval (own site/quote: card or PayPal; Etsy: see 6) | | Day 5-7 | Receipt, order sheet with species and dimensions |
| 6 Build | Timber selected, carpenter named, production photos at 50% and 100% | Workshop | 3-4 weeks | Two photo/video updates |
| 7 QC and balance | Final photos/video against the drawing; **60% balance due before packing**; customer can request fixes within 48 h | | week 5 | Approval step |
| 8 Pack and ship | Packed and photographed (packing video), joins the next container (LCL) or air batch if purchased | | week 5-6, then container closing | Tracking of the container leg |
| 9 Ocean/air, customs | Sea 6-8 weeks door to 3PL, air 4-5 weeks; customs entry by the broker | Forwarder, broker | | Milestone messages: departed, arrived US, cleared |
| 10 US delivery | 3PL ground shipping, curbside/first dry spot (customer carries it inside; white-glove optional) | 3PL | 3-7 days | Delivery date and tracking |
| 11 Follow-up | Care card, warranty card, day-3 check-in, photo/review request (not a condition of anything) | | after delivery | |

**Timeline promise: "10-12 weeks from approved deposit to your door" by sea (typical 10-11); "5-8 weeks" with the air premium.** These are ESTIMATES built from the logistics doc (production 3-4 weeks, consolidation 7-10 days, ocean 4-6 weeks, US devanning 5-10 days, receiving 2-3 days, ground 3-7 days). The date quoted at step 4 is computed from the next container closing date plus 9 weeks; if the customer's order misses a closing by less than 3 weeks we quote up to 14 weeks. Never write "3-5 days".

### Worked example (base plan)
Deposit on Mon 1 Feb 2027 (W19): production to 1 Mar, container 2 closes about 5 Mar, lands about 7 May, customer receives about 14 May = about 15 weeks. That is **too long for the promise** because container 2 is booked after the first stock: **so the first made-to-order customers in February are quoted "mid-May" (14 weeks) or offered air (about 22 Mar-5 Apr) at the premium.** Once containers leave every 6-8 weeks, 10-12 weeks holds. The promise is only made for a date we can compute.

## 4. Deposit, cancellation, changes (OWNER-SET draft; LW to review)
| Term | Draft |
|---|---|
| Deposit | 40% at approved design; 60% at QC approval before packing |
| Cancellation | Free within 48 hours of the deposit or before timber is cut, whichever is first; afterwards the deposit is non-refundable (materials and labour are committed) but can be credited to a standard stock item within 6 months |
| Changes | One free design revision before the deposit; after the deposit, dimension changes only within 10% and only before timber is cut (48 hours); finish changes until finishing starts |
| Final sale | Made-to-order and customised pieces are **final sale except for defects and damage in transit** (matches Mandir For Home's disclosed policy); disclosed before payment, on the quote and on the order sheet |
| **FTC Mail, Internet or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (16 CFR 435)** | The ship date stated on the quote must have a reasonable basis (default 30 days if no date is stated). **If we cannot ship by the stated date we send a delay notice with a new date and a free, easy way to cancel for a full refund; if the new date is more than 30 days later, we cancel and refund unless the customer agrees in writing; refunds within 7 business days (card: within one billing cycle).** Put the date in every message ([../../countries/us-legal/06-website-consumer-law.md](../../countries/us-legal/06-website-consumer-law.md)). "Ship date" in our case means the date the goods leave our control for the customer: **state both departure from Jodhpur and delivery-by**, and keep a buffer of two weeks |
| Missed delivery date | If delivery is more than 14 days after the promised date: USD 50 credit or 5% back (owner choice), never both; not a substitute for the legal cancel right |
| Price protection | Quote valid 7 days; tariff changes after acceptance are ours to absorb up to 5% of price (ASSUMPTION; **FAST**: duty changed three times in 2026) |

## 5. Etsy custom-order flow (ASSUMPTIONS; verify in Shop Manager)
1. Buyer uses **"Request a custom order"** or messages; we reply with the drawing and quote in Etsy Messages (keep everything on-platform: Etsy expects it and off-platform diversion is a policy risk).
2. We create a **reserved listing** for the buyer at the quoted price; Etsy collects the full price at checkout (a 40% deposit is achieved with a separate deposit listing then a balance listing; whether this is allowed and how Etsy Payments reserve/payout timing treats it must be confirmed).
3. Processing time set to the honest number (10-12 weeks); Etsy requires tracking uploaded and, for non-US sellers, **DDP** (mandatory since 9 Jul 2026; DHL not confirmed as a DDP carrier; whether a shop in India can ship from a US 3PL is **unverified**: ask Etsy first). Etsy fees (14.5% modelled) come out of the price: **the Etsy floor is 5-10 points higher than own-site** ([03](03-starter-catalogue.md)).
4. Purchase Protection and case handling: keep drawings, QC photos and the packing video as evidence.

## 6. Warranty and repair policy (DRAFT; attorney to review)

**The Wood Vintage 12-Month Limited Warranty (draft)**
- **What it covers:** for 12 months from delivery, defects in materials and workmanship in normal indoor household use: open or failed joints, loose or detached carved parts, finish that peels or blisters, hardware that fails, panels that split beyond normal seasonal movement.
- **What it does not cover:** natural variation in colour and grain; hairline seasonal movement; damage from humidity below 30% or above 60% relative humidity, radiators/vents, direct sun, water or heat; overloading (shelf loads stated on the listing); misuse, outdoor or commercial heavy use; alterations or repairs by others; carrier damage reported later than 48 hours; normal wear (scratches, dents in softer mango wood).
- **Remedy, in this order:** (1) **repair first**: we ship replacement parts or finishing kits (from the US 3PL spare stock in 2 business days, otherwise by DHL Express in 4-7 days) or reimburse a local carpenter up to USD 75 with a photo estimate; (2) replace the piece if repair is not possible; (3) refund only if neither can be done. We pay for parts and their shipping; the customer is responsible for assembly with the supplied instructions.
- **Damage in transit:** report within **48 hours** of delivery with photos of the carton, the label and the damage; keep the carton until we reply (first reply within 1 business day). Remedy as above. We hold the carrier claim.
- **Designation:** state "LIMITED WARRANTY" and make the full text available before purchase on every product page (Magnuson-Moss designation and pre-sale availability rules for written warranties on products over USD 15; [../../countries/us-legal/06-website-consumer-law.md](../../countries/us-legal/06-website-consumer-law.md)). No "lifetime" wording.
