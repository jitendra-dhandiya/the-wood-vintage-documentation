## 3. Operating rhythm: India time to US time
**Time zones.** IST is UTC+5:30. US Eastern is UTC-5 in winter (1 Nov 2026 to 14 Mar 2027) and UTC-4 in summer; Pacific is UTC-8/-7. **IST leads US Eastern by 10.5 h in winter (9.5 h in summer) and Pacific by 13.5 h (12.5 h).**

| Window (IST) | = US East (winter) | = US West (winter) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **19:00-23:00** | 08:30-12:30 | 05:30-09:30 | US morning: replies to overnight messages, order confirmations, marketplace inbox, Amazon/Etsy messages |
| **07:30-11:30** | 21:00-01:00 (previous day) | 18:00-22:00 | **US evening, when buyers browse and message**: live replies, quote follow-ups, community |
| 11:30-19:00 | 01:00-08:30 | 22:00-05:30 | Workshop hours, content production, deep work, no live chat (auto-reply with the promise) |
Staffing: two people, each on one window (4 hours a day, 7 days a week in the first 6 weeks after launch); Sunday reduced. WhatsApp Business away message states the two windows in US time.

**Service levels (ASSUMPTION; input H2):** first reply within **15 minutes in-window**, by 09:00 IST (about 22:30 US East the previous night) for messages received outside a window, **24 hours** maximum on Etsy and Amazon (Amazon requires reply within 24 hours; Etsy Star Seller tracks reply rate and speed); quotes within 48 hours; damage claims: first reply within 1 business day, resolution proposal in 3; refunds within 7 business days.

**Weekly cadence**
| Day (IST) | Task |
|---|---|
| Mon 10:00 | KPI review (30 min): orders, cost per order by channel, quote funnel, stock cover, damage, reviews; decide spend for the week ([07](07-budget-channel-mix-and-timeline.md) section 8) |
| Tue | Content shoot/edit (batch 5 reels, 20 pins); listing updates |
| Wed | Creator and designer outreach; community posts |
| Thu | Ads review; Amazon/Etsy report; container/3PL check |
| Fri | Lead sheet cleanup (source, segment, city, product, quote value, incentive used); order/carrier exceptions; weekly note to the owner |
| Monthly | Landed-cost recheck against actual 3PL and freight invoices; tariff/regulation check (FAST); tax nexus tracker (CPA); review the risk register ([10](10-risks-and-unknowns.md)) |

**UTM registry** (one sheet): `utm_source`=instagram/pinterest/facebook/amazon/etsy/whatsapp/referral/creator, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`=`us-<angle>-yyyymm`; a code or "ref:" tag on every quote so the source is known when the order closes off-site.

## 4. Contingency plans
| Event | Trigger | Response | Cost/impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Container delayed (port congestion, roll-over) | ETA slips more than 10 days | Notify pre-order customers with a new date and cancel option (FTC delay notice); reroute to air for priority orders at the customer's option; do not run stock ads | +1-3 weeks; pre-orders may cancel |
| Container lost/damaged | Insurance event | Cargo insurance (Institute Cargo Clauses A, about 0.4%); claim with photos and packing list; remake priority pieces | 4 weeks + deductible |
| **Customs hold or CBP exam (Lacey, CITES, valuation)** | Broker alerts | Documents ready (species sheet, purchase proof, invoice); broker responds within 24 hours; valuation defended at transfer price; if CBP rejects the low valuation, add about USD 25-40 duty per hero unit (margin -5 to -8 points) | days to weeks; storage fees |
| **Tariff/regulation change** (Section 232 on "bookshelf", Section 301 rate, de minimis) | any Federal Register/broker notice | Broker reclassifies; re-price or pause the affected SKU; the 1 Jan 2027 cabinet step-up to 50% is the known date | margin -10 points on cabinet-class lines |
| Amazon listing suppression, gating or account issue | notification | Fix the compliance document; fall back to 3PL + own site + Etsy for stock; do not depend on one account | lost channel share (18% of orders) |
| Etsy shop review/suspension (new India shop) | notification | Appeal with the workshop video/registration; keep the list and WhatsApp as a backup channel | up to 20% of orders |
| FBA check-in delayed or rejected | > 3 weeks | Sell the same units from the 3PL on the own site/Etsy; re-label; re-send | lost weeks of Amazon sales |
| Demand below the low case | W22 under 25 orders | Switch to the Lean run-rate; stop paid social; shift effort to community/B2B; delay container 2 | cash preserved |
| Hero stock-out | cover under 3 weeks | Made to order with dated promise; waitlist; air premium option | conversion drops |
| Damage rate above 10% in a month | claims log | Stop the SKU, redesign packing (thicker corner protection, honeycomb), re-drop-test, claim from insurance | replacement cost |
| Workshop capacity shortfall | container 2 cannot be built in 4 weeks | Split container 2, prioritise heroes; longer made-to-order dates | |
| **FX move** (INR 84-92 per USD) | monthly | recompute margins; +/-4% swings the coffee table by about 1 point | small |
| Freight quote above the model (USD 400 per CBM) | quote received | recompute; margin -3 to -5 points; price up 3-5% or drop S8/S10 first | |
| Payout hold/freeze (Payoneer, Amazon reserve) | notification | keep a USD 3,000 buffer; complete tax interview and identity checks in week 1-4 | cash |
| Chargeback or "not as described" dispute | notification | Evidence pack: order sheet, drawing, QC photos, packing video, tracking, delivery signature (over USD 500) | small |
| Negative review | posted | Reply within 24 hours; fix; never ask for removal in exchange for compensation | reputation |
| US helper unavailable | | Alternative: 3PL photo service (USD 5-10 per unit) or a freelancer | small |
| Owner or key staff unavailable 2 weeks | | Second person trained on the quote flow and the lead sheet; templates in one folder | |
