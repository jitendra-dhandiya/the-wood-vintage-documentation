## 1. Facts that anchor the segments
- About **5.2 million Indian Americans**, median household income **USD 151,200** (2023 ACS, Pew 2024; VERIFIED via [../../countries/us.md](../../countries/us.md) B2): a high-income, home-owning, festival-observing group. About eight in ten Asian-American Hindu households keep a home shrine and 95% of Indian-American Hindus celebrate Diwali (Pew 2012 via search summary, ESTIMATE).
- The mandir category is **served by US-stocked sellers with free ground shipping at USD 275-1,800** (Urli Utsav, The Mandir Store, Mandir For Home, Pooja Mandirs USA) and marketplace listings; the gap is **solid carved wood at USD 450-900**, custom size, provable provenance ([../../competitor-research/us-b2c-price-benchmark.md](../../competitor-research/us-b2c-price-benchmark.md) section 4).
- Demand signals we could see (small but real): Pinterest has idea pages for "Mandir in USA home", "Pooja mandir USA", "Pooja room ideas USA home DIY" (https://www.pinterest.com/ideas/mandir-in-usa-home/961787512231/, 2026-09-25); competitor Instagram accounts are small (Pooja Mandirs USA about 1,800 followers, Home Pooja Mandirs about 5,000, per search snippets): **the niche is under-served on social, not saturated (ESTIMATE)**. No search volumes were obtained.
- Mainstream: Etsy 86.6M active buyers and Wayfair 21.2M customers (Q3 2025, ESTIMATE); Pinterest 2026 predictions cite warm and carved wood (blog, ESTIMATE).

## 3. Primary versus secondary (decision)
1. **Primary: P1.** Highest fit (culture, product, customisation, festival calendar), lowest expected cost per order through community and referral, and the segment where competitors' "laminate/teak finish" weakness is strongest. It also carries **Diwali 2027**.
2. **Secondary: P2**, entered through Pinterest, Etsy and Amazon with the mirror, jaali and table range. Larger, but a Wayfair-priced field; we win only on craft, custom and honest dates.
3. **Test:** P4 (30 personal messages), P5 (5 studios), P6 (2 temples) at zero paid cost.
4. **Order of tests:** angle A (P1: "solid wood mandir made in Jodhpur, sized in inches, duties paid"), then B (P2: carved mirror and jaali), then C (P4: "custom furniture from India, one crate, one date").

## 4. Messaging by segment
| Segment | Headline (test) | Proof lines | Do not say |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | "A solid wood mandir, hand-carved in Jodhpur, sized for your wall" | Species named, dimensions in inches, drawing sent before you pay, workshop video, duties included, ships from our US warehouse in 3-7 days | Any religious efficacy claim ("brings prosperity"); "Vastu approved" unless the owner can support it; "teak" for mango/sheesham |
| P2 | "Carved by hand in Rajasthan. Delivered to your door." | Carved mirror close-ups, joinery, real room photos, damage promise, honest lead times | "30% cheaper than Wayfair"; "sustainable/eco" without documents |
| P3 | "A gift with a maker's name, on time" | Ship-by date shown, gift note, damage promise | "Delivered in 3-5 days" |
| P4 | "Custom furniture from a Jodhpur workshop: one price, one date" | Trade sheet, sample kit, past projects (real), lead-time table | Fake project photos |

## 6. Channel targeting specs (starting points; test and record)
### 6.1 Meta (Instagram + Facebook)
- **Caution:** Meta removed detailed targeting options tied to sensitive topics (religion, ethnicity) in 2022 (ASSUMPTION from memory; verify in Ads Manager). Do not depend on religion or ethnicity targeting; **let geography, language and creative do the targeting**.
- Location: US metros with large Indian-American populations: NY/NJ (Edison, Jersey City), San Francisco Bay Area (Fremont, San Jose), Dallas-Fort Worth (Plano, Frisco), Chicago (Naperville, Schaumburg), Houston (Sugar Land), Atlanta (Alpharetta), Seattle-Bellevue, Washington DC (Fairfax, Ashburn), Boston, Raleigh-Durham, Phoenix, Austin (ASSUMPTION; confirm audience size per DMA in Ads Manager).
- Age 28-55, all genders; **language: English plus Hindi, Gujarati, Telugu, Tamil, Punjabi where the option exists**; interests to test (whichever are still available): Diwali, Indian cuisine, Bollywood, Indian wedding, interior design, home improvement, Etsy, Pinterest, Wayfair, West Elm.
- **Advantage+ audience / broad with strong pooja creative** as one ad set; **lookalikes** from the customer and lead lists once 100+ contacts exist; **retargeting**: 30-day video viewers, product-page visitors, Instagram engagers, quote-form openers.
- Objectives: **Sales/Leads with click-to-WhatsApp and lead forms** for P1; traffic/catalogue sales for Amazon/Etsy is not measurable by Meta (the Meta pixel cannot see marketplace checkouts): send P2 traffic to the site product pages or a "buy on Amazon/Etsy" link and measure with UTMs and platform reports.
- Budget per ad set: USD 12-20 a day; kill an ad after USD 40 without a lead; scale only above the [07](07-budget-channel-mix-and-timeline.md) rules.
- **Special ad categories do not apply** to furniture.

### 6.2 Pinterest
- Keywords and interests: pooja room decor, home mandir, Indian home decor, mandir design for home, carved wood mirror, jharokha mirror, boho console table, warm wood decor, entryway bench; Home Decor and Furniture interests; **actalike audiences** from site visitors and buyers.
- Formats: standard and video pins, shopping/catalog pins once the feed exists (needs the site product feed, a dev item). Home décor CPC about USD 0.50-0.80 (ESTIMATE); **purchase window 21-30 days**, so start pinning in week 6, six to ten weeks before stock lands. Boards and pin specs in [../social/11-us-diaspora-and-mainstream-playbook.md](../social/11-us-diaspora-and-mainstream-playbook.md) section 6.

### 6.3 Google
- **Merchant Center free listings** (US feed, once product data with USD price, shipping and returns exists) and Search on **exact/phrase**: "wood mandir for home", "pooja unit for home", "carved wood mandir", "sheesham wood coffee table", "handmade jharokha mirror", "custom furniture from India". Location US; exclude AK/HI/PR; household-income top tiers as an observation (not exclusion) audience; **start only after 15 quote leads or first sales are recorded** (conversion tracking) and the site passes Gate C.
- Amazon: Sponsored Products auto then manual/exact on "pooja mandir for home", "wooden mandir", "carved wood mirror"; Etsy Ads USD 5 a day on the three best listings after 20 views a day.

## 7. Sizing and measurement method
Use the Ads Manager audience-size tool per metro and Pinterest's audience insights to get monthly reach; multiply by the 0.8% click-to-order and 1.2% CTR (input E) to see the orders a metro could yield per USD 1,000; do not buy a metro whose reach is under about 100,000. Record every lead's segment (P1-P6), city, channel and product in the lead sheet from day one; **after 30 orders re-rank the segments by cost per order and contribution, and rewrite this file.**
