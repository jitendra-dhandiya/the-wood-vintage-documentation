## 0. How the gates work
Four gates, in order. **Nothing that spends money on goods before Gate 0; nothing ships before Gate A; no listing or ad goes live before Gate B; no ad sends US visitors to thewoodvintage.com before Gate C.** Target dates assume decisions on Fri 9 Oct 2026 (the base plan in [07](07-budget-channel-mix-and-timeline.md)).

| Gate | Purpose | Target date (base) | Blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 Decide** | Cost sheet, species, cash, quotes: the model is real | Fri 9 Oct 2026 | the production order |
| **A Ship** | Goods can legally leave India and enter the US | Fri 6 Nov 2026 (container handed to the forwarder) | the container |
| **B Sell** | Marketplaces, policies, photos and support are ready | Fri 20 Nov 2026 (Etsy live), Fri 15 Jan 2027 (Amazon live) | any listing or ad |
| **C Site** | thewoodvintage.com is safe and honest for US visitors | Fri 27 Nov 2026 (quote-and-content mode), later checkout | ads to the own site |
