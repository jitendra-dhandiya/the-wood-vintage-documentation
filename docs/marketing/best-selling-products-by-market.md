## 1. Executive summary

1. **We cannot see real sales.** No marketplace publishes units sold. We use proxies: rating/review counts (Flipkart listings, Wooden Street product ratings), how many SKUs a seller stocks per category (supply depth), "Bestseller" tags, and search-trend blogs. Treat every "demand" score as an ESTIMATE; the first 30 orders will replace it with real data.
2. **India - lead with light-to-medium solid-wood pieces that ship as a single parcel or small freight and carry the owner's customisation:** coffee tables, bedside tables, wall/bookshelves, carved wall panels and mirrors, mandir units, and the kitchen-wood gifting set. **Hold beds, wardrobes and jhulas** until the process (packing, freight partner, delivery dates) is proven - they are the heaviest, highest-CAC and highest-return items.
3. **USA pilot - small décor under 10 kg** (trays, boards, spice/storage boxes, carved panels, toys). Price anchors: mango-wood tray USD 24.99-34.99 at World Market (India-made); Etsy boards USD 45-180 (blog ESTIMATE). Shipping economics decide everything (see unit-economics-model.md section 6).
4. **UAE - what the local importers stock:** sideboards/cabinets (295 SKUs at Art & Craft), side tables (127), coffee tables (73), consoles (58), TV units (51), mirrors (51), dining tables (50) - all mango/sheesham/acacia, AED 1,000-3,500. A Jodhpur maker fits as **supplier to those importers**; consumers there expect COD and instalments.
5. **Best first hero for India:** a **"Living-room starter set" combo**: coffee table + 2 bedside/side tables + carved mirror (all under ~22 kg each), priced with the combo tool (decision 0037) - it lifts average order value above the INR 4,999 free-shipping threshold and spreads CAC.

## 2. Scoring method (all three markets)

Score each candidate 1-5 on five criteria; weighted score out of 100 = sum(weight x score / 5).

| Criterion | Weight | What we looked at |
|---|---|---|
| Demand evidence | 30 | Rating counts, SKU depth, festival/wedding relevance, trend reports |
| Margin headroom at the target price | 25 | unit-economics-model.md (contribution after freight, gateway, RTO, CAC) |
| Shippability | 20 | Weight, breakage, volumetric size, CITES 10 kg rule for export |
| Customisation fit | 15 | Can size/finish/carving be tailored (decision 0039) without new tooling |
| Competitive headroom | 10 | How crowded/price-anchored the category is (5 = open) |

**Every score is our judgement (ASSUMPTION).** Change the weights and re-rank.
