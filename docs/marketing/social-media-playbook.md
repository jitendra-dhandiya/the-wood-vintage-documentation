# Social media playbook - The Wood Vintage (Instagram + Facebook, India first)

Date: 2026-09-25. Status: research and plan (development on hold; no code changed). Owner: marketing workstream.
Part of the Phase-2 deliverables in [marketing-workstream-plan.md](marketing-workstream-plan.md). Companion plan (written by another team): `first-100-orders-plan.md` (offers, budget, channel mix, 100-order arithmetic) - this playbook covers the social channels only and defers to it for budget and offer decisions.
Evidence tags used everywhere: **VERIFIED** (read on the page/tool on 2026-09-25), **ESTIMATE** (secondary source, methodology unknown), **ASSUMPTION** (our reasoning; owner input needed), **HEURISTIC** (best-practice rule of thumb - not a platform rule).

## 0. The answer in one page
1. **Positioning:** "Built by our own carpenters in Jodhpur, made to your size." The moat is the workshop and the custom brief-to-delivery story. National competitors run catalogue/lifestyle feeds (Wooden Street 503K, Pepperfry 401K, Urban Ladder 227K followers - VERIFIED 2026-09-25); the Jodhpur peer we could read (Woodsala, ~21K) posts product reels and shows no visible maker story.
2. **Goal of the account:** qualified WhatsApp conversations -> quotes -> advance-paid orders. Followers are secondary. India D2C is the target for the first 100 orders; US (hybrid Etsy/Amazon + B2B) and UAE come later (market-selection.md).
3. **Cadence:** 4 reels + 1-2 carousels + 1 static per week + daily Stories + a 30-minute community routine. About 12-16 h/week for 1-2 people.
4. **Content:** 6 pillars (Workshop, Made For You, Know Your Wood, Real Homes, Honest Pricing, Seasons) x the Hook -> Story -> Proof -> CTA reel structure; 39 reel scripts; a full 30-day calendar starting Mon 28 Sep 2026; a 90-day roadmap around Navratri (11 Oct), Dhanteras (6 Nov), Diwali (8 Nov) and the wedding season restarting after 20 Nov.
5. **Funnel:** bio link + WhatsApp prefilled message with `ref:` codes -> free sketch/quote in 24 h -> advance -> build updates -> delivery -> review/UGC. The site's quote form (decision 0034) and WhatsApp CTA are the destination.
6. **Growth:** own-network seed, Reels SEO in the caption/audio/text, collab posts, daily 10-comment routine, nano/micro creator seeding (ESTIMATE rates: nano ~Rs 1-15K, micro ~Rs 8-85K per deliverable), interior-designer trade programme, Pinterest and YouTube Shorts repurposing, compliant merit-based giveaways.
7. **Paid:** only after the readiness gate (real WhatsApp number, payment keys if selling online, reply SLA). Start with Click-to-WhatsApp and website quote-form traffic; boost only proven organic reels; guardrails and stop-loss rules in 06.
8. **Measurement:** first-touch UTM (`utm_source/medium/campaign` only) is what the site sees; WhatsApp chats need `ref:` codes and labels; weekly review template and UTM registry in 08.
9. **Compliance:** every claim (species, solid, warranty, price %) must be true; ASCI/#ad rules for barter too; UAE permit and FTC rules for later pilots; consent for carpenters and customers (09).
10. **Biggest risks:** (a) slow replies waste paid spend; (b) unverified claims ("solid teak", "30% cheaper", "lifetime warranty"); (c) content that needs real customers before they exist - do not fake it; (d) placeholders still in the product (WhatsApp number 919876543210, payment keys, demo Instagram handle - decisions 0030/0034, in.md).

## 5. What we could not verify (be honest)
- Reel-level performance (views/engagement) of any benchmark account was not visible; "what performs" is therefore observation plus HEURISTIC, to be validated by our own test log (08).
- Follower counts come from page fetches on 2026-09-25 (rounded); older search snippets differed (e.g. Wooden Street 406K vs 503K).
- Home-decor-specific influencer rates, Instagram hashtag volumes for our keywords, Meta CPC for furniture in India, UAE competitor accounts, TikTok status in the USA, and the primary ASCI guideline text were not found. Numbers used are labelled ESTIMATE with sources in 10.
- Festival and muhurat dates come from panchang/astrology sites that sometimes disagree; recheck the month before use.
