## 2. Segment profiles

Legend: Meta = targeting spec; Google = keyword themes (from [../../seo/keyword-map-india.md](../../seo/keyword-map-india.md), relative tiers, no volumes); price sensitivity L/M/H.

### 2.1 New-home setters (PRIMARY)
- **Who / persona "Neha, 31, Gurugram":** moved into a rented or bought 2BHK; wants a warm, wood, "not cookie-cutter" look; hates delivery delays and the "fake discount" feel of big brands; compares Wooden Street, Pepperfry, IKEA and Instagram brands.
- **Pains:** will it fit my room? is it real wood? when will it come? what if it arrives damaged? no showroom to touch it.
- **Triggers:** lease/possession date, housewarming date, festival, salary/bonus, a friend's flat on Instagram.
- **Where:** Instagram reels and saves, Pinterest, Google for specific items, WhatsApp (family groups), YouTube home-tour videos.
- **Message:** "Made for your room, delivered on the date we promise. One honest price." Proof of process, not adjectives.
- **Offer:** free sketch/dimension check on WhatsApp within 24 hours; C1 and C2; FIRST100 (5%).
- **Entry product:** C1 (24,999), S7 coffee table (11,999), S1/S2 mandir; first order often INR 5-12k.
- **Price sensitivity:** M (buys on trust + value; will compare against IKEA/Amazon for cheaper items).
- **Proof needed:** real photos of your pieces in real homes, workshop video, the written warranty, a sample dimension drawing, wood sample offer, 3+ real customer messages/photos (until then: your own room and friends' rooms with permission and a caption that says so).
- **Meta:** Objective Engagement -> Messages (WhatsApp) or Leads -> WhatsApp; age 26-42, gender all; geography Phase A: Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Ajmer, Delhi NCR (Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad); Phase B (after freight quotes are known): Bengaluru, Mumbai/Navi Mumbai/Thane, Pune, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad; language English + Hindi; Advantage+ audience ON with suggestions (interests: home decor, interior design, home improvement, furniture; a "recently moved" behaviour may exist for India - verify in the Ads Manager UI, not assumed); placements Advantage+; creative-led (3 hooks x 1 format). Exclusions: past buyers, pincodes where freight makes the product non-viable (decision 0015).
- **Lookalike seeds:** none for the first 4-6 weeks (need 100+ quality seeds: advance-paid buyers and qualified WhatsApp leads exported from the sheet as a hashed phone list; do not create from 20 people).
- **Google:** custom furniture india; made to order furniture india; sheesham coffee table; carved wooden console table; wooden temple for home (see 2.2); housewarming gifts wooden; exclude cheap/used/olx/plywood/engineered.

### 2.2 Families buying a mandir (PRIMARY, co-lead)
- **Persona "Meena, 46, Jaipur, and her son, 27, Bengaluru":** replacing a plywood wall temple, or setting up a new pooja space; prefers solid wood, carving, small sizes for flats.
- **Pains:** quality vs the INR 150-500 engineered wall temples on Flipkart (about 47,500 ratings in the top 40 listings, per [../best-selling-products-by-market.md](../best-selling-products-by-market.md)); size for the wall/niche; delivery breakage.
- **Triggers:** Navratri 11 Oct, Diwali 8 Nov, griha pravesh, marriage in the family.
- **Where:** Facebook and Instagram (older women/men on Facebook: keep Facebook placements on), WhatsApp family groups, Google, YouTube, Pinterest.
- **Message:** Hinglish + Hindi creative: "Aapke ghar ke size ka mandir, Jodhpur ke karigar ke haath se" (approve wording with a native reviewer). Show the carving hand, not a rendering.
- **Offer:** custom size (height/width within the niche), carving tier, finish; C2; DIWALI26 in window.
- **Entry product:** S2 (9,999) and S1 (15,999).
- **Price sensitivity:** M-H (anchored by cheap engineered temples; the pull is solid wood + carving + size).
- **Proof:** carving process video, real photo in a real home, wood/finish sample, written dimensions and warranty.
- **Meta:** age 30-55, same geographies, language Hindi + English, **no religion-based interests** (Meta removed detailed targeting tied to sensitive attributes such as religion in 2022 - ESTIMATE, verify in the UI); let the creative self-select; separate ad set so the budget is judged on its own; Facebook placements on.
- **Google (Search, exact/phrase):** wooden temple for home; carved wooden mandir; solid wood pooja mandir; mandir for home wooden; jaali panel wooden. Negative: cheap, wall hanging plastic, MDF, olx, second hand, "for car".

### 2.3 Interior designers, architects, contractors (SECONDARY, warm)
- **Persona "Karan, 34, designer, Jaipur":** 8-15 projects a year; needs custom sizes, one reliable workshop, a trade rate, documents (GST invoice), a date he can promise his client.
- **Pains:** vendors who miss dates, fake wood, no drawings, no communication.
- **Triggers:** a project needing a piece not available off the shelf; client budget cuts.
- **Where:** Instagram (they follow craft accounts), Pinterest, WhatsApp, Justdial/Google Maps, LinkedIn (organic; not researched here).
- **Message:** "Your drawing, our workshop. Dimension-accurate, dated, invoiced."
- **Offer:** trade code (10% or 6% commission, [05](05-coupon-strategy.md)), sample kit (finish swatches, a small piece), workshop visit, 24-hour sketch turnaround.
- **Entry product:** custom jaali panels (S3/S4), consoles, benches, C7.
- **Price sensitivity:** M (they care about margin for themselves and reliability).
- **Proof:** a lookbook PDF (6-10 real pieces), a delivered-on-time record after 10+ orders, workshop video.
- **Targeting:** direct outreach lists (60-100 firms in Jaipur, Jodhpur, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru built from Instagram, Google Maps, Justdial); Meta lookalike not applicable; follow the 5.4 designer outreach in [../social/05-growth-plan.md](../social/05-growth-plan.md).
- **Google:** custom furniture manufacturer for interior designers; furniture manufacturer Jodhpur (branded, low volume; use for GBP not ads).

### 2.4 Gifters: Diwali, housewarming, wedding (SECONDARY, seasonal)
- **Persona "Pooja, 38, HR manager":** needs 8-30 gifts for Diwali; or a couple's housewarming gift under INR 5,000.
- **Pains:** generic gifts, delivery dates, tacky packaging.
- **Where:** Instagram, WhatsApp broadcast, Google ("Diwali gifts wooden", "housewarming gifts wooden").
- **Offer:** C3 (4,999), C4 (5,999), C5 (11,999), engraving, corporate quantity quote (10+).
- **Timing:** Diwali order window closes 28 Oct for ready stock; wedding season from 20 Nov.
- **Price sensitivity:** H for gifts under INR 3,000; M for INR 4,999+ where the free delivery and packaging matter.
- **Meta/Google:** organic-first; Google Merchant free listings and Shopping tab (needs product feed W-14); no cold paid on C4 (organic-only tag).

### 2.5 Rajasthan locals (SECONDARY)
- Jodhpur, Jaipur, Udaipur, Ajmer: walk-in trust, workshop visits, same-day quotes, lower freight, word of mouth (ASSUMPTION: 15-25% of the first 100 orders). Message: "Come see it being made." Meta geo-fence to these cities at INR 300-500/day; GBP with real photos; local creators; referral code.

### 2.6 Cafés, homestays, boutique hotels, small offices (B2B, later)
- Persona: owner of a 30-seat cafe in Jodhpur/Jaipur, or a homestay owner; needs benches, tables, jaali dividers, trays; GST invoice; 3-6 week fit-out timeline; often refers others.
- Offer: C9 spec + custom finish match; site visit measurements; quote per seat.
- Where: direct visits/DMs, Google Maps, Instagram (tag venues you have furnished), industry groups on WhatsApp.
- Meta: none in Phase 1. Google: "cafe furniture manufacturer Jaipur", "homestay furniture wooden", "restaurant wooden furniture custom" (Low volume, Win tier).
- Sizing: count venues on Google Maps in your three cities; each is 1-3 orders; 4 orders in 16 weeks is assumed.

### 2.7 Newlyweds and wedding-season buyers (from 20 Nov)
- Two customers: the couple (setting up) and the gift givers. Content and offers: C5, C1; wedding planners and creators; "griha pravesh after wedding".

### 2.8 NRI gifting and furnishing back home
- Persona: NRI in UAE/US/UK buying for parents' home in India; trust via WhatsApp video call and family-side contact; payment by international card or transfer (a payment method gap: gateways refuse non-INR today, OPN-01). Handle as quote-only with a bank transfer or payment link. Meta geo-targeting of NRIs is not proposed for Phase 1.

### 2.9 WFH/study
- Desk + shelf (C6) for professionals and parents of students; price sensitive vs engineered desks; margin only fair (S11 12%); test as one ad in Jan, not before.

## 3. What to test first (weeks 4-12)

Order of tests (one variable at a time, [../social/06-paid-social.md](../social/06-paid-social.md) 6.5):
1. **Segment:** new-home setters (C1-led creative) vs mandir families (S1/C2-led) at equal budget in the same cities. Decision at 30 qualified conversations per arm or INR 15,000 each.
2. **Geography:** Rajasthan+NCR versus the six-metro list, judged on cost per qualified conversation **and** freight cost per order.
3. **Language:** Hinglish vs English (mandir creative first).
4. **Entry price:** S2 at 9,999 vs S1 at 15,999 as the lead product.
5. **Warm channels:** designer outreach (send 30 lookbooks; count replies and site visits) - not an ad test but the highest-leverage experiment.

## 4. How to size each segment without inventing numbers (fill the sheet in week 1)

| Segment | Free method | Record |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2 (Meta) | Ads Manager audience definition: potential reach for the geographies, ages and Advantage+ suggestions above (free, in the campaign set-up) | Reach per city cluster; check the Base scenario needs about 89,000 impressions/week ([07](07-channel-mix-and-budget.md) section 5), which at frequency about 3 means about 30,000 unique people/week - an audience under about 300,000 will saturate |
| 1, 2 (Google) | Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account), keyword-map themes | Monthly searches for each theme by city/state; expected clicks = volume x CTR at position x share; no volumes exist in our docs |
| 3 | Build the list: firms per city from Instagram, Google Maps, Justdial | Count of firms; replies; orders per firm |
| 4 | Google Trends for gifting terms (Aug-Nov); last year's competitor gifting pages | Weekly interest by state |
| 5 | GBP insights; local Instagram followers; the owner's own contacts (count) | Own network reachable on WhatsApp (target 150-300 people) |
| 6 | Google Maps count of cafés and homestays in Jodhpur/Jaipur/Udaipur | Count; contacts made; quotes |
Existing ESTIMATES in the docs to keep in perspective (not to size our market): 35 lakh+ weddings recorded in India in 2024 (retailer blog, [../best-selling-products-by-market.md](../best-selling-products-by-market.md) 3.1); home décor/furniture the second most planned festive purchase category at 51% (survey blog, same file); India furniture market USD 25-31B ([../../countries/in.md](../../countries/in.md)). These say the market is large; they do not say how many people we can reach with our budget.
