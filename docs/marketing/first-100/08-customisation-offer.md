## 1. Why productise it

Woodsala's "customisation" is mostly polish; Wooden Street offers wood/finish/size but service and delivery dates are the top complaints ([../../competitor-research/india-market-price-benchmark.md](../../competitor-research/india-market-price-benchmark.md) section 6, VERIFIED). A visible menu with honest adders, a fixed process and a delivery date we keep is the opening the competitors leave. It also reduces the "quote, then silence" failure of high-ticket chat sales, and removes the price-surprise objection ("no price signal for custom work", gap CUS-04). The site today has size/finish variants (decision 0039) and a quote form (decision 0034); everything else in this menu is handled on WhatsApp and the spec sheet.

## 2. Customisation menu with price adders

## 3. The 6-step process: brief -> design -> quote -> advance -> build -> delivery

Timelines are defaults (ASSUMPTION; inputs A3-A6 in [01](01-owner-input-sheet.md)); the promise we can publish is the one the workshop confirms.

| # | Step | What happens | Owner | Time | What the customer sees / receives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Brief** | Customer sends the WhatsApp message or quote form (item, city, budget, room size or photo, date needed). Sales desk answers the five qualifying questions ([../social/07-community-and-sales.md](../social/07-community-and-sales.md) 7.2) | Sales desk | First reply within 15 minutes (business hours) | Friendly reply, checklist for measurements, a measurement how-to |
| 2 | **Design consult** | 15-20 minute WhatsApp video call (or voice + photos): confirm dimensions, wood, finish, carving tier, hardware; the designer sketches the piece to scale (hand sketch or Canva); door/lift width check for delivery | Designer / owner | Consult within 24 hours of a complete brief; sketch within 48 hours | Dimensioned sketch, finish swatch photos, optional swatch kit |
| 3 | **Quote** | Written spec sheet PDF: dimensions, species, finish, hardware, price (itemised: base + adders), delivery cost, lead time, payment schedule, cancellation and change-order terms, warranty, quote validity (7 days) | Sales desk | Within 24 hours of the consult | One page they can forward to a spouse or designer |
| 4 | **Advance and slot** | Customer approves the spec sheet and pays the advance (UPI/bank/payment link; later on-site), receives a GST invoice and a slot confirmation with a delivery date range | Sales desk + accounts | Same day | Receipt, slot, delivery window, named contact |
| 5 | **Build** | Cut, carve, polish, pack; progress photos/video at 25%, 50%, 90% | Workshop lead | Décor 12-18 working days; furniture 21-30 working days; ready stock 3 working days | Three updates; a "we are late by N days" message **before** a date slips, with the new date |
| 6 | **Delivery and sign-off** | Balance payment before dispatch; packed-piece video; tracking link; delivery check on video call; sign-off photo; day-3 review request, day-30 care tip, referral note | Dispatch + sales desk | Transit 4-7 days (default) | Tracking, care card, warranty card, an easy way to report a problem |

**Consultation channels.** WhatsApp video is the default; a Calendly/Google Calendar link in the WhatsApp Business profile lets customers pick a slot (free tools; CUS-01/LOW-03 in the gap tracker ask for this on the site later). Workshop visits welcome in Jodhpur for Rajasthan customers.

**Samples and swatches.** Finish swatch kit (three woods x three finishes, small pieces): free with a quote of INR 12,000 or more; otherwise INR 299, refunded against the order (owner decision). Wood sample on request for quotes above INR 25,000. Cost from the samples line in [07](07-channel-mix-and-budget.md).

## 5. Written warranty and repair policy - draft for the owner to approve

> **The Wood Vintage - 12-month workshop warranty and repair promise (draft)**
> We repair, not replace. For 12 months from the delivery date we will repair, at our cost, any defect in the workmanship or in the wood of a piece we made, such as a joint that opens, a drawer or door that is not built to the agreed size, a finish that peels or bubbles under normal use, or a crack caused by the wood not being properly seasoned.
> **How to claim:** message us on WhatsApp [number] with your order number and photos or a short video. We reply within 48 hours and give you a repair date. Repairs are done in our workshop or, where possible, at your home by our technician or by a local carpenter we brief and pay. We pay the freight for repairs we accept.
> **If a piece cannot be repaired** we will replace the part or the piece, or refund the paid price by mutual agreement.
> **What is not covered:** damage from water, humidity or direct heat that is not normal for the room; termite or insect damage that starts outside the piece; cracks or movement that are the natural behaviour of wood within normal limits (we tell you what to expect for your city on the care card); accidents, misuse, overloading, wear from ordinary use; changes or repairs by someone else without asking us; commercial use beyond the agreed specification (6 months for cafes, hotels and offices).
> **Transit damage:** please film the unboxing or delivery and tell us within 48 hours; we will repair or replace the damaged part.
> **Our promise on honesty:** we state the wood of every piece (for example sheesham, mango) and tell you where engineered board is used. This warranty is in addition to any rights you already have under law.
> Owner and lawyer: confirm the period, the exclusions, the freight rule and the last sentence before publishing. Do **not** publish a "lifetime" warranty unless you will honour it for life ([../positioning-and-usp.md](../positioning-and-usp.md) section 6, item 2). Woodsala gives 1 year; Wooden Street "no replacement"; Urban Ladder voids warranty if not assembled by its experts (VERIFIED in the benchmark).
