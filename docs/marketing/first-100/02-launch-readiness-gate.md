**Development is on hold.** So the gate is split into two levels so that money is not blocked by code:

- **Gate A - "WhatsApp-led launch" (no code needed).** Everything is configuration (Admin > Settings, Homepage Builder, CMS pages, product active flags), content, photography or a business account. When Gate A is green you may spend on **Click-to-WhatsApp and quote-form-traffic ads only**. Orders are closed on WhatsApp (advance by UPI/bank transfer or a gateway payment link) and logged in the lead sheet.
- **Gate B - "Website-sales launch" (needs the dev freeze lifted for the code items).** Required before ads whose objective is online checkout, before catalogue/retargeting/Performance Max, and before the site is allowed to take orders on its own. Until B is green, ready-stock items on the site may be sold only if payment keys are live and the order-confirmation gap is covered by a manual WhatsApp confirmation.

Gate A target date: **Fri 16 Oct 2026** (end of week 3). Gate B target: **Fri 13 Nov 2026** (end of week 7), earlier if development resumes now. Both are ASSUMPTIONS driven by photography (A4) and owner availability.

Effort: S = under a day, M = 1-3 days, L = a week or more. Owner column: **O** = owner, **M** = marketing lead, **C** = carpenter/workshop lead, **CA** = accountant, **D** = developer (only when the dev hold is lifted), **P** = photographer/videographer.

## Pass/fail rules

1. **No paid spend** until A1-A17 are ticked. A4/A5/A6 are not negotiable: they are the difference between a trusted small brand and a suspicious one.
2. **Boosts of organic posts** (under Rs 5,000 total) are allowed once A1-A4 and A14 are done, to test creative before the full gate.
3. If the SLA (15 minutes in hours) is missed on two consecutive days, pause all ads for 48 hours (stop-loss rule in [../social/06-paid-social.md](../social/06-paid-social.md) 6.6).
4. Re-run the gate on every campaign change of objective (WhatsApp -> website sales requires B1-B5).

## What the gate deliberately does not require

A Meta Pixel for CTWA ads, reviews on product pages (none exist yet; reviews are a P2 build once real reviews exist), a payment-in-parts option, AR/3D preview, hi-fi customer portal, US/UAE payments or copy. These are logged in the gap tracker with P1-P3 priorities.
