## 3. Festival calendar and realistic Diwali capacity (owner inputs A9-A10)

Dates are given by the owner brief: **Navratri Sun 11 Oct, Dhanteras Fri 6 Nov, Diwali Sun 8 Nov 2026, wedding season after 20 Nov.**

**The honest position:** the gate closes on 16 Oct, and made-to-order lead times are 12-18 working days for décor and 21-30 for furniture plus 4-7 days transit ([01](01-owner-input-sheet.md) A3-A6). Diwali is a **ready-stock and gifting festival** for us, not a made-to-order one.

| Product class | Last order date for arrival by Diwali (default) | Arithmetic | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ready stock (pre-built mandirs, jaali, mirrors, kitchen sets, C3/C4 gift sets) | **Wed 28 Oct** (Rajasthan/NCR: Mon 2 Nov) | 3 working days to dispatch + 4-7 days transit = arrives 4-7 Nov; Dhanteras (6 Nov) is only reachable for orders up to about 26 Oct | Requires the ready stock from A9 |
| Made-to-order décor (custom size/finish/carving) | **Fri 16 Oct** with a confirmed 12-working-day lead time; earlier if 18 days | 16 Oct + 12 working days (Mon-Sat) = dispatch 30 Oct + 4-7 days transit = 3-6 Nov | Effectively only orders from the owner's own network placed before the gate closes; do not advertise |
| Made-to-order furniture | **No Diwali promise** | 21-30 working days from any date after 28 Sep lands after 8 Nov | Sell for wedding season and new year instead |
| Post-Diwali (9 Nov) and wedding season | Ready stock and gifts: rolling; furniture: quote against the next month | | Gifting content from 20 Nov |

**Capacity for Diwali (owner to set A1/A2/A9):** default plan = 45 ready-stock units built in weeks 1-4 (10 x 2 ft mandirs, 10 jaali panels, 15 kitchen gift sets, 10 mirrors; about INR 1.1 lakh at default ex-factory costs) plus at most 12 custom pieces. If the carpenters can only build fewer, cut the ready-stock list to the highest-margin items first (jaali, mandir 2 ft). **Do not publish a Diwali delivery promise until the workshop confirms in writing the quantity and the dates.** Scarcity messaging ("only N in stock") is allowed only with the real count.

**Content by festival:** Navratri greeting (11 Oct); Diwali order-by countdown (12-28 Oct, "order by 28 Oct for Diwali delivery" on ready-stock items only); Dhanteras and Diwali greetings and "how we make it" content with no discount; post-Diwali "new home for the new year" and wedding-season gifting from 20 Nov.

## 4. Weekly operating rhythm - who does what

Roles (owner to name people, input A7): **Owner** (decisions, approvals, price and capacity); **Sales desk** (2 people, WhatsApp/DMs/quotes/follow-ups); **Marketing lead** (content, ads, reports, outreach); **Workshop lead** (capacity, lead times, progress updates, packing); **Accounts** (advances, invoices, gateway, coupons ledger). One person may hold more than one role in the first weeks.

| When | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| Daily 09:30 (15 min) | Owner + workshop lead + sales desk | Stand-up: capacity this week, lead time to quote today, ready-stock count, orders to dispatch, any slip to announce |
| Daily 10:00-20:00 (IST, 7 days) | Sales desk | Answer WhatsApp/DMs inside SLA, qualify, quote, log in the lead sheet with source and label |
| Daily 12:30 and 19:30 | Marketing lead | Post or story per the calendar; reply to comments for 60 minutes after posting |
| Daily 20:15 (10 min) | Marketing lead | Ad spend, cost per conversation, any ad to pause; update the KPI sheet's daily row |
| Daily | Workshop lead | Progress photos/videos for orders at 25%, 50%, 90%; message any slip before it happens |
| Daily | Accounts | Advance received? invoice sent; balance requests for orders 3 days from dispatch |
| Monday (45-60 min) | Owner + marketing lead + sales desk | Weekly review with the template in [../social/08-measurement.md](../social/08-measurement.md) 8.3; decide 3 actions; compare with section 2 targets |
| Tuesday | Marketing lead | Creative test set-up or refresh; new ads from last week's winning organic reels |
| Wednesday | Marketing lead + owner | Designer outreach: 10 new DMs/calls and 5 follow-ups; workshop visit invitations |
| Thursday | Marketing lead | Shoot/edit batch (reels, carousels), UTM links tested in incognito, captions with UTMs |
| Friday | Owner + workshop lead + accounts | Production/delivery review; coupon ledger (usage report, cancel PENDING orders older than 48 hours); floor-price check on redeemed orders; ready-stock update |
| Saturday | Workshop lead + marketing lead | Workshop content day (with carpenter consent); customer delivery photos |
| Sunday | Sales desk (rota) | Cover SLA; light content |
Hours: about 12-16 hours a week for content and community ([../social/04-calendar-and-roadmap.md](../social/04-calendar-and-roadmap.md) 4.1), plus 3 hours a day per sales-desk person at Base volumes.

## 6. Contingency plans

| Trigger | Diagnose | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **Leads below target** (chats or quotes under 60% of the section 2 targets for 2 weeks) | Where does the funnel leak? Reach -> click-through (creative/hook) -> chat (CTA, prefilled message) -> qualified (audience, price shown) -> quote (desk speed) -> order (offer, proof, price) | Low click-through: new hooks, more real-home shots. Chats but few qualified: add the price band and city in the ad text; tighten geography. Quotes but few orders: offer a swatch kit or video call, tighten quote turnaround, review price against Woodsala, check proof. Also lean on warm channels: personally message 30 more contacts, 20 more designers |
| **CAC above ceiling** (INR 3,000 for 14 days and 5+ orders) | Which ad set/creative/product? Is the spend going to products whose break-even CAC is below the CAC (trays, boards)? | Pause those ad sets; lead with combos and T1 products; move budget from Meta cold to retargeting and creators; drop geography with high freight; fall back one scenario if it persists |
| **Production backlog** (lead time above the promise, or backlog above 10 working days) | Capacity input A1/A2 too high? too many custom pieces? timber delay? | Stop scaling ads; change the quoted date on the site and in ads the same day; move furniture quotes to the next month; add a second shift or a subcontract carpenter for standard pieces; do not accept new custom furniture orders until under 10 days |
| **Damage or complaint** | Packaging, courier, or workmanship? | Follow the warranty; pause the affected SKU's ads; fix packing (corner guards, foam, crate); log in the damage sheet; feed real numbers into input B10 |
| **Payment or checkout failing** | Gateway keys, approval, network | Take advances by UPI/payment link on WhatsApp; pause website-sales campaigns; keep CTWA |
| **SLA breach 2 days in a row** | Staffing or volume | Pause ads 48 hours; add a person; reduce daily budget by 50% |
| **Timber price rises above INR 1,800/cft** | Recompute [03](03-starter-catalogue.md) floors | Raise prices toward the recomputed floors; drop S9/S11/S12 from the hero list; pause combos that no longer clear the floor |
| **Coupon abuse or margin leakage** | Usage report shows repeated addresses or orders below floor | Deactivate the code; cancel PENDING orders; tighten limits ([05](05-coupon-strategy.md)) |
| **Negative public comment or fake-review attempt** | | Respond within 1 hour with facts; never delete honest criticism; never post fake reviews |
| **Week-8 checkpoint fails** (under 12 cumulative orders in Scenario C) | | Fall back to Scenario B spend, re-test proof and offer, extend the plan by 4 weeks; do not "make up" the gap by discounting margin-negative SKUs |
