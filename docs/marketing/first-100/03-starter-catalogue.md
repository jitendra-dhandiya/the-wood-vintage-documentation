## 1. Which SKUs, and why these

Selection rule: (1) high score in the best-sellers ranking; (2) light enough to ship as a parcel or a small consignment (under 40 kg for the launch set); (3) clears the E1/E2 floors at default inputs; (4) carries a visible customisation (size, carving, finish, engraving); (5) photographs well on a phone. Three tiers:

| Tier | Meaning | Ads allowed? |
|---|---|---|
| **T1 Hero** | Clears the floors with room to spare and has a strong story | Yes, paid + organic |
| **T2 Support** | Clears the floors only with organic/WhatsApp/referral acquisition or inside a combo | Organic, combos, retargeting; no cold ads |
| **T3 Quote-only** | Not shown with a buy button; "made to order on enquiry" with a written quote and a floor price | No ads until the process is proven (order 30+) |

**Do not discount beds, TV units, bedside tables, wardrobes, sofas.** At default inputs they sit at or below zero after paid CAC (table in section 4). They are shown only as quote-only pieces at their floor prices.

### What this table says, in plain words

1. **Only the "30% below brand-tier selling price" story is true for coffee table, consoles, mirrors, side tables, mandirs (vs Woodsala) and dining.** For trays and cutting boards we are at par, for bookshelves and desks the brand tier is not like-for-like (engineered wood or mixed sizes), and we are always above Flipkart sellers. Never write "30% cheaper" in any ad; write "factory-direct from Jodhpur, one honest price" ([../positioning-and-usp.md](../positioning-and-usp.md) section 4).
2. **Woodsala is the only rival with the same wood and city**; against it we are 26-44% lower on the launch SKUs above, which is either a real cost advantage, or a sign that our cost inputs are too low. **Check S1, S3, S5, S7, S9 costs first** - if the carpenters' real ex-factory cost is above the "Max cost for 20% margin" columns of the unit-economics model, raise the price toward Woodsala.
3. **T1 items clear the floors by 12-26 points of price headroom** at INR 1,500/cft timber. **At INR 2,200/cft timber** most of the range collapses (table in section 4); at INR 900/cft most of it is healthy. Timber price is the swing factor; get the real number before you print any price.
4. **Small kitchen items are not standalone products.** They exist for gifting, add-on baskets and combos; do not run cold ads on them.

## 5. What to photograph first (real photos only - gate item A5)

Two shoot days at the workshop plus one styled-room day. Every SKU gets at least the six frames below; the mandir, jaali, mirror, coffee table and tray are also the US/UAE listing photos later ([../best-selling-products-by-market.md](../best-selling-products-by-market.md) section 6).

| Frame | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 Hero, clean background, natural light | Product tile, Merchant/marketplace feed |
| 2 In a real room (a friend's or the owner's home), with everyday objects | Scale and aspiration (avoid over-styling) |
| 3 Scale shot with a person or a known object, and a dimension overlay | Removes the "will it fit" doubt |
| 4 Carving/grain close-up with a hand or a ruler | Proof of solid wood and craft |
| 5 Joinery/underside and finish swatch beside the product | Wood we name, wood we prove |
| 6 Packed, crated, ready to ship | Damage anxiety |

Priority order for the first shoot day (fit the day to what the workshop can finish): S1 mandir 2.5 ft; S2 mandir 2 ft; S3 and S4 jaali; S7 coffee table; S8 end table; S5 mirror; S10 bookshelf; S13-S15 kitchen set as a group and singly; S9 console; S16 wall shelves; S11 desk; S12 bench; S6 round mirror. Also film: 60-90 s workshop video (carpenters with consent), one time-lapse of a mandir carving, one packing video, and finish swatches on three woods. Video and stills feed the reel library ([../social/03-reel-library.md](../social/03-reel-library.md)).

Photographing beds, TV units, dining sets: only when a real customer order is being built (then the build itself becomes proof content).

## 6. Catalogue set-up notes (config only)

- Each SKU: sizes and finishes through the size system (decision 0039): mirrors W x H presets, tables Small/Medium/Large, custom builder for made-to-size. Add "Made to order - ships in N working days" using [01](01-owner-input-sheet.md) A3-A5 (state the real number).
- T3 items: create as products with a price "on quote" text in the description and the quote CTA as the only route, or leave them off the site and quote on WhatsApp; owner's call ([04-combo-strategy.md](04-combo-strategy.md) rule 1 forbids using T3 pieces in combos except with an owner-approved floor).
- Deactivate every other seed product and combo before launch (gate A5). Seed combos (Kitchen Starter Set 5,499, Living Room Accent Set 15% off, Gifting Trio) must be replaced with the combos in [04](04-combo-strategy.md) because their contents are seed products.
- GST: the site uses 18% net-of-GST logic for furniture; décor may be 12% or 5% (CA to confirm). If décor is 12%, every décor margin improves by about 5% of price.
