# First 100 orders - plan (India D2C)

Date: 2026-09-25 (Fri). Owner-facing entry point; the detail lives in ten files under [first-100/](first-100/). Companion: [master-growth-plan.md](master-growth-plan.md) (the single document to run the business from). Development is on hold: everything here is documentation, no code was changed. Evidence tags: VERIFIED / ESTIMATE / ASSUMPTION / OWNER-SET. **Every cost and conversion number is an ASSUMPTION until you replace it in [the owner input sheet](first-100/01-owner-input-sheet.md).** The margin numbers were computed with the unit-economics formulas in [unit-economics-model.md](unit-economics-model.md); the funnel with the ranges in input sheet section D.

## The plan in ten lines

1. **Market: India (domestic D2C) for all 100 orders.** USA = later hybrid pilot (small décor, marketplace/B2B), UAE = research only ([../countries/market-selection.md](../countries/market-selection.md)).
2. **Do not spend a rupee before gate A** (real WhatsApp/phone, fake content removed, real photos, made-to-order policies): target Fri 16 Oct 2026 ([02](first-100/02-launch-readiness-gate.md)).
3. **Launch catalogue: 16 SKUs + 4 quote-only pieces.** Heroes: carved mandirs, jaali panels, coffee/end tables, bookshelf, kitchen-wood sets; beds, TV units, bedside tables are quote-only at floor prices ([03](first-100/03-starter-catalogue.md)).
4. **Nine combos** (Housewarming Living Set 24,999; Mandir Corner 21,999; Cook's Gift Set 4,999; Diwali Gifting Trio 5,999; Newlywed Home Gift 11,999; Study Nook 27,999; Entryway Set 28,999; Apartment-in-a-Box 57,999; Cafe/Homestay Kit 62,999), all above their floors ([04](first-100/04-combo-strategy.md)).
5. **Eight coupon codes** (FIRST100, DIWALI26, SHAADI26, referral give/get, WhatsApp closing, abandoned-quote nudge, creator, trade), one per order, never on combos ([05](first-100/05-coupon-strategy.md)).
6. **Audience:** primary = new-home setters (27-42) and mandir-buying families in Rajasthan and Delhi NCR first; secondary = designers/architects (warm, high leverage) and gifters; B2B and NRI later ([06](first-100/06-target-audience.md)).
7. **Budget: Scenario C (Base) in stages** - INR 1.25 lakh per 4 weeks at steady state, released as the stop/scale rules allow; about INR 3.6 lakh over 16 weeks; about 96 orders by week 16 and 100 in week 17 (about 18 Jan 2027) in the base case, range 59-132 ([07](first-100/07-channel-mix-and-budget.md)).
8. **Honest economics:** paid CAC (base INR 2,650) leaves about INR 900 of the INR 3,573 average contribution; the plan is cash-negative by about INR 2.4 lakh over 16 weeks. Organic/referral/B2B supply about 35 of the 96 orders.
9. **The offer is "made to your room, one honest price, a delivery date we keep, repair-first warranty"** - not "30% cheaper" ([08](first-100/08-customisation-offer.md)).
10. **Diwali (8 Nov) is a ready-stock and gifting festival for us**: last ready-stock order 28 Oct; no made-to-order furniture promise ([09](first-100/09-roadmap-and-operations.md)).

## The four numbers that decide everything

| Number | Default | Why it matters | Where to replace |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timber INR per cubic foot | 1,500 (range 900-2,200) | At 900 most launch SKUs are healthy; at 2,200 only the jaali panels stay above 15% after paid CAC | input B1 |
| Cost per order from paid ads | INR 2,650 (range 425-17,200 by funnel case) | Break-even is INR 3,573 per average order; the range shows why the first INR 10-25k is a test, not a campaign | inputs D1-D6 |
| Organic + referral + B2B orders in 16 weeks | 35 (21-46) | The least-evidenced number; also the cheapest orders | input D12 |
| Workshop capacity per month | 40 décor + 10 furniture | Base nears it at peak, Growth exceeds it | inputs A1-A2 |

## Order of work (first two weeks)

1. Owner fills the P0 rows of the input sheet with the carpenter team (one afternoon) and gets two freight quotes; the margins are recomputed.
2. Gate A items A1-A4 and A6-A8 (config and content) are done in week 1; photography in weeks 1-3.
3. Personal WhatsApp outreach to 150-300 contacts starts as soon as 9-12 posts are live (week 2).
4. Pre-build the Diwali ready stock in weeks 1-4 if the owner accepts about INR 1.1 lakh of stock.
5. Decision meeting on the open owner decisions listed in [master-growth-plan.md](master-growth-plan.md) section 5.

## What this plan does not do
Export own-site, paid ads before the gate, discounting margin-negative SKUs, price-only competition, US/UAE creative, or any code change. See [master-growth-plan.md](master-growth-plan.md) section 6.
