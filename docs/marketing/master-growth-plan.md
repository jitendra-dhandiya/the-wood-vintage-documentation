## 6. What we are deliberately NOT doing yet

- **Export own-site (US/UAE/UK/EU/AU/CA):** payments, tax, freight, tariffs and CITES/EUDR are unresolved; USA is a marketplace/B2B pilot after order 30-50; UAE stays research.
- **Paid ads before the readiness gate** (real WhatsApp, fake content removed, real photos, made-to-order policies).
- **Discounting or bundling margin-negative SKUs** (beds, TV units, bedside tables, wardrobes, sofas); no "sale" theatre or fake MRP.
- **"30% cheaper" claims**, competitor-named comparisons, fake reviews, fake urgency, "lifetime" warranty, "teak" for sheesham/mango.
- **Price-only competition** against Flipkart factory sellers.
- **Cold ads on small kitchen items** (break-even CAC INR 550-790) and on the organic-only Diwali Gifting Trio.
- **Performance Max, catalog ads and lookalikes** before the catalogue, pixel/feed and 100+ quality seeds exist.
- **Agencies and large influencers**; celebrity designers.
- **COD on made-to-order furniture.**
- **New code or engineering work** (the hold stays except gate B items the owner approves).
- **Expanding beyond about 20 SKUs** before order 30; upholstery, sliding wardrobes, storage beds.
- **A Hindi/regional SEO push**, marketplace (Amazon.in/Flipkart) listing, PR.
- **Stacking coupons** or discounts on combos.
