## 7.2 The sales process (customised orders)
```
Enquiry (DM / comment / WhatsApp / quote form)
  -> 1. Qualify (5 questions: item, room size, city, budget range, needed-by date)
  -> 2. Consult (photos/video call, measurements, wood + finish choice)  [label: Brief received]
  -> 3. Sketch + quote (drawing, spec sheet, price, timeline)            [label: Quote sent]
  -> 4. Follow-up (D+1, D+3, D+7 max, then park)
  -> 5. Confirm + advance payment ({X}% advance; written spec)           [label: Advance paid]
  -> 6. Production updates (photos/videos at 25/50/90%)                  [label: In production]
  -> 7. Pre-dispatch check + balance payment + delivery slot            [label: Ready-Dispatch]
  -> 8. Delivery + fitting + sign-off photo                             [label: Delivered]
  -> 9. Day-3 review/UGC request, Day-30 care tips, referral ask        [label: Review asked]
```
Rules: no price without room size unless it is "starting from"; never promise a delivery date before checking workshop capacity; put every commitment in writing (spec sheet PDF: dimensions, wood species, finish, hardware, timeline, price, payment schedule, cancellation and warranty terms); take the advance only after the spec is agreed; customised pieces are typically non-returnable after production starts (ASSUMPTION - owner and legal to fix policy and reflect it on the site policy pages).
Payment options: UPI/bank transfer for advance (until gateway keys are live in production - in.md); COD for standard items only where allowed; partial-advance COD for high-return-risk pin codes (in.md notes ~26% COD return rate, ESTIMATE). Share the payment link/UPI ID only from the business number.

## 7.5 Handling negative comments and reviews
1. **Do not delete** genuine complaints (it looks evasive); do hide clearly abusive/spam comments and report them.
2. **Respond within 2 hours (business day) publicly, briefly, humbly, then move to WhatsApp.** Template: "Sorry for the trouble, {Name}. We want to fix this. Please message order no. to {Number}; {Owner name} will call you today." Never argue or expose customer data in public.
3. **Classify:** (a) genuine complaint -> fix and follow up; (b) misunderstanding -> answer politely with facts; (c) troll/competitor/spam -> one polite reply if needed, then ignore/hide; (d) defamatory/harassing -> screenshot, report to the platform, seek legal advice if needed.
4. **Close the loop:** after the fix, ask if they'd update the review; reply again publicly "Resolved - thank you {Name}."
5. **Internal:** log each complaint with cause (damage, delay, mismatch) and add a fix to the process (packaging, lead-time accuracy, spec sheet) - review monthly.
6. **Google review replies:** thank, apologise where due, invite offline resolution, keep under 100 words; never offer money for removal.
7. **Crisis rule:** if 3+ similar complaints appear within a week, pause paid ads and fix the cause first.

## 7.6 Review and testimonial collection
- Timing: day 3 after delivery (fitting done) and again at day 30 (long-term use).
- Ask via WhatsApp (script above); include the short Google review link and QR on the invoice/care card.
- Ask for: (a) Google review, (b) 15-30 s video reaction, (c) photo of the piece in the home. Get written consent for reposting; save it.
- Do not offer money for positive reviews, do not gate reviews (do not ask only happy customers to post), and never write fake reviews. Consumer-protection rules in India treat fake/incentivised reviews as unfair practice (ESTIMATE; e-commerce review guidance exists - verify with a lawyer). 
- Display: screenshot reviews to Highlights ("Reviews"), pin the best one, add to site `TESTIMONIALS` section (decision 0030; content is admin-editable).
