## 4.1 Recommended weekly cadence (1-2 person team)
| Item | Per week | Time (ASSUMPTION, 1.5 people part-time) |
|---|---|---|
| Reels | 4 (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat) | 30-60 min editing each if shot in batches |
| Carousels | 1-2 (Tue; Thu alternates) | 45 min each in Canva |
| Static/photo post | 1 (Sun) | 15 min |
| Stories | 3-6 frames every day, 5 days min | 10 min/day, shot in the workshop as things happen |
| Facebook | cross-post all reels + 1-2 native posts | 15 min via Meta Business Suite scheduling |
| Community routine | 30 min/day (see 05) | 3.5 h |
| WhatsApp replies | within SLA (see 07) | 30-60 min/day, includes quoting |
| Planning/analytics | 1 h Monday (weekly review, 08) | 1 h |
| Total | | ~12-16 h/week |
Why this number: a reel-led plan of 4/week is sustainable with 2 shoot sessions per month (see 09) and matches the level a 30-day plan needs (17 reels). Posting more is not a virtue; consistency for 12 weeks is. (HEURISTIC) Minimum viable plan if overwhelmed: 3 reels + 1 carousel + daily stories.

**Soft-launch rule (HEURISTIC):** before you tell anyone the account exists (family, customers, WhatsApp status), have 9-12 posts live (5 reels + 3 carousels + 3 statics) and 4 Highlights filled, so a new visitor sees a real brand. Schedule these on Day 1 (2 posts) and continue as below; announce publicly on Day 4 with a WhatsApp status and personal contacts.

**Best times to post:** unknown for this account; start with 12:30-14:00 and 19:30-21:30 IST (HEURISTIC) and read Insights > Followers > Most active times after 2 weeks.
