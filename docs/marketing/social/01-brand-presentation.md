## 1.1 The one-line brand idea (use it everywhere)
**"Built by our own carpenters in Jodhpur, made to your size."** (ASSUMPTION: true for every product; confirm with owner. If some items are bought-in, say

## 1.6 Verification and trust signals
- **Blue tick:** Meta Verified is a paid subscription with ID + profile-match checks and price/availability by country that we could not verify (ASSUMPTION - check Instagram > Settings > Meta Verified). Do NOT buy in the first 30 days; consider after the website has real payment keys, GST invoicing, and ~10 orders. National brands here are verified (VERIFIED 2026-09-25); Woodsala's peer account is verified as well.
- **Trust stack to show publicly (only what is true):** workshop address + Google Maps pin; GST-registered invoice (state "GST invoice provided" only when Razorpay/GST setup is live); named carpenters; wood-source facts (mandi/supplier, seasoning time - owner to state); written warranty terms (**do not copy "lifetime warranty"** used by competitors unless the owner will honour it in writing); return policy for custom pieces; customer reviews with photos; response-time promise equal to the site setting.
- **Never:** fake follower/like purchases, fake reviews, stock photos passed as own work, "sold out" or countdown urgency that is not real (decision 0034 spec rule).

## 1.8 Brand voice and tone

**Voice in one line:** a skilled Jodhpur carpenter who happens to be a good storyteller - warm, plain, specific, proud of the work, never salesy.

| Trait | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| Craftsman-plain | Say what it is: "sheesham, 1.5 inch thick, hand-finished" | Adjectives without proof ("premium luxurious world-class") |
| Warm and respectful | "Aap", first names, "hamari team" | Over-familiar slang or pressure ("Hurry!!") |
| Honest about trade-offs | "Solid wood is heavier and costs more than engineered wood - here is when each is right" | Trashing competitors by name |
| Proud, not boastful | Show the joinery, the mistakes we fix, the carpenter's name | Claims we cannot prove ("No. 1", "best in India") |
| Bilingual by default | English headline + Hinglish caption line; Devanagari for festival and family posts | Forced Sanskritised Hindi or Google-translated text |

**Tone dial by moment:** Educational = calm and clear. Process = quiet and observant (let the sound of the tools talk). Offers = friendly and factual (dates, terms). Complaints = humble and fast (see 07). Festival = warm and celebratory.

### Do / Don't examples
| Situation | Don't | Do |
|---|---|---|
| Reel hook | "Best furniture in Jodhpur! Buy now!!" | "This bed frame took Ramesh-ji 6 days. Here is every joint." |
| Wood claim | "100% solid teak furniture" (unless it is) | "Made from sheesham (Indian rosewood). Seasoned {N} months. Here is how you can check." (only if true) |
| Price | "Cheapest in India" | "Factory-direct means no showroom rent and no middleman margin. Here is where the money goes." (gate the exact % until measured - the 30% claim is still an ASSUMPTION, marketing-workstream-plan.md) |
| Custom | "Fully customisable! DM for price" | "Tell us the wall length and how many people sleep in the room. We reply with a drawing and a quote within {1 business day}." |
| Caption CTA | "Link in bio to buy" only | "Want this in your size? WhatsApp 'QUOTE' to {number} - we reply with sketch + price." |
| Negative comment | Delete or argue | "Sorry for the trouble. Please WhatsApp order no. to {number}; {name} will call you today." (see 07) |
| Hinglish | "Hamara furniture bahut hi awesome quality ka hai" | "Har joint haath se, har piece aapke size ka." |
| Urgency | "Only 2 left!! Sale ends in 1 hr" (fake) | "Diwali build slots: {N} left for delivery before 6 Nov. Real lead time is {X} days." (only real numbers) |
| Festival | Random generic greeting graphic | Workshop clip of the piece being finished for a real family + a warm 1-line wish |

**Emoji rule (HEURISTIC):** max 1-2 per caption, wood-related or neutral; none in customer-support replies about complaints.
**Words we use:** "hand-finished", "made to your size", "our carpenters", "sheesham", "seasoned", "joinery", "brief", "sketch". **Words we avoid:** "cheap", "luxury" (unless pricing supports it), "best", "guaranteed" (unless in written policy), "antique" (unless it is), "pure wood" (ambiguous - say the species).

## 1.9 Visual identity rules for Reels, posts, stories, thumbnails
Palette: walnut `#3B2314`, copper `#A0693A`, cream `#FFFCF5`. Contrast (computed by us, WCAG formula): walnut on cream ~15:1 (great); cream on walnut ~15:1 (great); copper on cream ~4.5:1 (OK for large text/accents only, not small body text); copper on walnut ~3.4:1 (large text only). Never put copper small text on walnut.
Fonts: Cormorant (Garamond) for headlines, a clean sans (Inter / Poppins / DM Sans) for body and Devanagari (Noto Sans Devanagari / Poppins Hindi). Import custom fonts into Canva/InShot; CapCut availability of custom fonts varies by version (ESTIMATE) - if not, export text overlays as PNG from Canva.

| Asset | Rules |
|---|---|
| Reels (9:16, 1080x1920) | Hook text in first 1 s, top third, max 6 words, cream on a soft walnut scrim or walnut on cream; keep captions inside the central safe area (avoid the bottom ~20% and right edge where UI overlays sit - HEURISTIC); burn-in subtitles in Hindi/Eng (many watch muted); end card 1.5 s: wordmark + "Made in Jodhpur" + CTA line; consistent colour grade: warm, natural light, no heavy orange filter; never shake - use tripod/gimbal clamp. |
| Feed posts (4:5, 1080x1350) | Carousel slide 1 = hook headline (Cormorant, big) over best photo; last slide = CTA card (walnut background, cream text). Photos: 1 hero, 1 detail (joinery/grain), 1 in-situ. |
| Stories (9:16) | Interactive sticker in every second frame; consistent bottom-left sticker "TW" monogram small; max 5-7 frames per day. Use link sticker with UTM (`utm_medium=story`). |
| Thumbnails / covers (Reels cover 1:1 crop safe zone for the 3-column grid ~ centre 4:5) | Cover text <= 5 words, same font, same position; 1 of 3 covers pattern (see above). Grid check: zoom out to phone size - text must be readable at 100 px. |
| Logo use | Monogram in profile photo and as small corner mark; wordmark on end cards only; do not stamp a large watermark across product video (reduces watch-through - HEURISTIC). |
| Photography | Natural window light; show scale (person, book, mug); dust-free, calm background; at least one detail macro of grain/joint; never over-sharpen; include a real room, not only studio white. |
| Sound | Original: workshop ambience (sanding, hammer, chisel), light voiceover in Hinglish/English. Music: only licensed/library tracks (see 09-production-and-compliance.md). |
| Accessibility | Subtitles on all speech; alt text on feed posts (Advanced settings) with product, wood, colour, room. |
