## 1. The bet, in one paragraph
Instagram and Facebook start at 0 posts, so **content is proof, not reach**: real Jodhpur workshop footage, dimensions in inches, packing and drop-test video, and honest dates. Two audiences, one workshop: **diaspora families** (mandir and pooja space, festivals, parents' approval) and **mainstream design buyers** (warm carved wood, global/boho, entryway and living-room styling). The goal of an account is **qualified conversations and marketplace clicks**, not followers. Competitor niche accounts are small (about 1,800 and 5,000 followers, per search snippets), so a focused account can lead the niche (ESTIMATE).

## 6. Pinterest strategy
- **Why:** high-intent, long-life discovery; home décor CPC about USD 0.50-0.80 and CPM about USD 2-5, purchase window 21-30 days (trackbee.ai, stackmatix.com 2026, ESTIMATE); "Mandir in USA home" and "Pooja mandir USA" idea pages exist (pinterest.com/ideas, 2026-09-25, VERIFIED existence, no volumes). Start in week 6, months before stock.
- **Set-up:** business account; **claim the website**; enable rich pins (Product) once the site or listings expose price and availability; connect Etsy if eligible (ASSUMPTION); catalog/feed later.
- **Boards (10):** Home mandir ideas; Pooja room in an apartment; Compact mandir designs; Carved wood mirrors; Jharokha and jaali wall decor; Entryway console and bench; Warm wood living room; Housewarming gift ideas; Mango wood care and guides; Workshop and craft. Each board description carries 3-5 keywords.
- **Pins:** vertical 2:3 (1000 x 1500 px); text overlay with the keyword ("Carved wood mandir for a small apartment"); title = keyword phrase, description = 2-3 sentences with dimensions, wood and "ships from our US warehouse"; **fresh pins weekly**, multiple pin designs per product; link to the specific page (site, Etsy or Amazon), UTM on site links; 10-15 pins a week; video pins from the reels.
- **Ads:** from week 18 at USD 105 a week; audience = keyword targeting (see [../us-first-100/06-target-audience.md](../us-first-100/06-target-audience.md) 6.2), actalikes later; measure saves, outbound clicks, and orders by UTM.
- **KPIs:** monthly views, saves, outbound clicks, orders (target 20k views a month by week 16, ASSUMPTION).

## 7. Facebook groups, WhatsApp, YouTube
- **Facebook groups** (city Indian communities, housewarming groups, interior/DIY groups): join as the business owner, read the rules, **ask the admin before any post**, give value (sizing guides, "how to check solid wood"), never spam links; a few groups ban all commerce: respect that.
- **WhatsApp Business:** click-to-WhatsApp from Instagram/Facebook and site; catalogue with real prices; **TCPA: WhatsApp marketing to US numbers needs prior express written consent**; replies to customers who message first are fine. Broadcasts only to opted-in lists.
- **YouTube:** evergreen: workshop tour, "how to choose the size of a home mandir", "solid wood vs laminate", assembly videos (which double as Amazon listing videos), "pooja room design for a small apartment". Shorts repurpose reels.

## 9. Reviews: how to ask, compliantly
| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials rule (16 CFR 465, effective 21 Oct 2024)** | No fake, purchased or AI-invented reviews; no review suppression or "review gating" (asking only happy customers); **incentives may not depend on the sentiment or content of the review**; insider reviews must be disclosed; civil penalties per violation (US law-firm summaries; the legal doc lists it, ASSUMPTION on details) |
| **Amazon** | Use the built-in **Request a Review** button (Buyer-Seller Messaging) or Amazon's automated request; **no incentives, no discounts or gifts for reviews, no asking for positive reviews, no packaging inserts that ask for positive feedback, no reviews from friends/family, no review-for-refund**. Vine (needs Brand Registry; fees apply, ASSUMPTION) is Amazon's compliant route for early reviews; Amazon's own policy pages govern (verify in Seller Central) |
| **Etsy** | Etsy sends the review reminder; a polite message after delivery is allowed; **no incentives, no exchange of discounts for reviews, no review manipulation**; reply publicly to every review |
| **Own site / off-platform** | Ask every buyer after delivery with the same neutral message; publish all reviews, good and bad; verified-buyer badge; if a thank-you gift is given, give it to everyone who replies regardless of rating and do not condition on review text (LW to confirm) |
| **Photos** | Ask for a photo of the piece in the home with written permission to reuse it; "customer photo" only with consent |
| Message template | "Hi {name}, your {piece} should have arrived. If you have a minute, an honest review helps other families choose. Anything wrong? Reply here and we will fix it first." (contains no promise of a reward) |
| **Do not** | Ask friends/family in India to review; use seeded testimonials; reuse the seed "testimonials" that still sit on the site (gap C1 in [../us-first-100/02-launch-readiness-gate.md](../us-first-100/02-launch-readiness-gate.md)) |

**FTC disclosure (Endorsement Guides 16 CFR 255 and 16 CFR 465):** any material connection (payment, free product, discount, family/business tie) must be **clearly and conspicuously disclosed**: "#ad", "#gifted", or the platform's paid-partnership label, placed in the first line, visible on video (spoken and on-screen), not buried in hashtags, not "#sp"/"#collab" alone. The brand is responsible for monitoring creators; keep contracts and screenshots. Do not ask creators to write reviews on Amazon/Etsy.
