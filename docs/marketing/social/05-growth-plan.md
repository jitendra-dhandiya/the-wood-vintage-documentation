## 5.2 Instagram levers (India), ranked by cost-effectiveness for a fresh account (HEURISTIC ranking)
1. **Own network -> first followers and first orders.** Send a personal WhatsApp message (not a broadcast spam) to 100-200 people who could plausibly buy or refer: "We've started sharing how we make furniture in Jodhpur. Follow for a look; if you or someone you know needs a bed/sofa/dining table, we build to size." Ask for a save/send of the R01 reel. Warm audiences give the first watch-time and sends that the algorithm reads (watch time, sends per reach are named signals, ESTIMATE).
2. **Reels SEO:** keyword in the first caption line, spoken keyword in first 3 s, on-screen text, location Jodhpur, alt text, cover text (02 2.5).
3. **Collab posts:** Instagram lets up to 5 accounts co-author a post so it appears on all profiles and reaches all audiences (ESTIMATE, Hootsuite/Buffer). Use with (a) the wood supplier, (b) an interior designer using our piece, (c) a customer, (d) a Jodhpur heritage hotel/cafe furnished with our piece, (e) a creator. Send the Collab invite only after they agree; accept reciprocity.
4. **Comments on others (30-min routine below):** the single free way to be seen by target buyers in the first 3 weeks.
5. **Sends:** design each reel's "send-worthy" line; end with "send this to whoever is buying a bed". (Sends per reach = strongest reported signal for non-followers, ESTIMATE.)
6. **Trial/test reels and A/B hooks:** for each idea, post 2 hooks on different days (reshoot only the first 2 s) and compare 3-second hold and watch-through (08). (HEURISTIC; Instagram "trial reels" feature exists for showing reels to non-followers first - check in-app availability, ESTIMATE.)
7. **Hashtags:** 3-5 per post (02). They aid categorisation, not reach.
8. **Trending audio:** optional and only per the rules in 02 (licensing).

### Daily 30-minute community routine (Mon-Sun; use a timer)
| Min | Task | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10 | Reply | Reply to all comments and DMs on your posts; move buying intent to WhatsApp using the scripts in 07; heart + reply within 60 min for the first 2 hours after posting (HEURISTIC) |
| 10-20 | Comment out | 10 thoughtful comments (>= 8 words, not emojis) on posts by: interior designers, home-renovation creators, new-home/housewarming pages, city lifestyle pages (Jaipur, Jodhpur, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru), wedding planners, woodworking creators. Pick target lists in a sheet of 60 accounts, rotate 10/day. |
| 20-25 | Follow and story reactions | React to Stories of followers/customers; reply to story poll voters with a DM thanks |
| 25-30 | Log | Copy any enquiry to the lead sheet with source; note top 3 comment questions -> next reel topics |
Do NOT: buy followers/likes, use follow/unfollow bots, mass-DM strangers, or copy-paste the same comment (Instagram limits inauthentic behaviour; accounts can be restricted - HEURISTIC/policy risk).

### Comment and DM funnel -> WhatsApp
Trigger (comment keyword or story reply) -> auto/quick reply in DM with 1 question -> move to WhatsApp with prefilled message `ref:IG-REEL-<id>` -> qualification -> sketch/quote (07). Manual for the first 30 days; consider automation later only after checking Meta's rules and opt-in (24-hour messaging window rules apply on Meta messaging - verify current policy, ESTIMATE). Target: >= 60% of serious DMs moved to WhatsApp within the first 3 messages.

## 5.3 Giveaways - done compliantly (India)
Purpose: followers + UGC, not the primary growth engine. Run at most once per quarter, only after ~500 organic followers, with a small useful prize (a wooden decor piece or a cutting board worth Rs {2,000-5,000}; cost is the landed cost to us).
Rules to follow (ESTIMATE/verify with a lawyer or CA; not legal advice):
- **Skill/creativity contest, not a lottery.** India treats lotteries restrictively (banned in about half the states, per a Lexology summary - ESTIMATE). Use "post a photo of the corner you want to fill and tell us the size" judged on a stated criterion, or a quiz, and pick a winner by merit. Random draws should have a clear mechanism, no purchase required, and a free entry route; if in doubt, keep it merit-based.
- **No purchase necessary; no payment to enter.** Do not require buying or paying a fee.
- **Follow platform promotion rules.** Instagram/Facebook forbid using their platform features misleadingly (e.g., requiring to tag people who did not opt in, or requiring personal-timeline sharing on Facebook - see Meta's promotion guidelines; not fetched, verify at https://www.facebook.com/policies_center). State plainly that the promotion is not sponsored/endorsed by Instagram.
- **Post written terms** (linked): eligibility (India residents 18+), start/end dates, judging criteria, prize description, delivery timing/shipping conditions, how the winner is announced, use of entries (UGC permission), data privacy line.
- **Fair, real prize;** announce the winner publicly; ship within 14 days; keep proof.
- **Tax on prizes** and any TDS obligations: confirm with a CA before running (ASSUMPTION).
- **Never** run a "giveaway" to get fake reviews or to hide an ad without disclosure.

## 5.4 UGC and customer content
- Ask every delivered customer (day 3 after delivery) for: 1 photo + 1 short video of the piece in their home + a Google review (script in 07).
- Permission ask in the same message: "May we share your photo/video on Instagram? We'll tag you or keep you anonymous." Save the consent message. (Required to repost - 09.)
- Reward (not payment for a review): a small thank-you (polish kit, coaster set) delivered regardless of review content; do not condition on a *positive* review (Indian consumer law and platform policies treat incentivised reviews carefully - ESTIMATE; verify).
- Feature "Home of the Week" every Friday; store all UGC in a labelled folder (09 naming).

## 5.5 Referral programme (simple, uses existing coupon engine)
Coupon engine facts (decision 0035): one coupon per order, code case-insensitive, percentage/fixed/free-shipping, min-order, per-user limits, first-order-only, usage tracked per order; public coupons appear as "Available offers". Combos (0037) can be priced as bundles.
Program: "Refer a friend: they get {Rs X / Y%} off, you get a free {decor piece} after their delivery." Implementation: create one code per referrer in Admin (no code change), `userLimit=1`, `usageLimit={N}`, with a `REF-` prefix (e.g., `REF-ANITA`). Order records `couponCode`, so referral orders are attributable without a new system (ASSUMPTION: admin can create codes with these limits - confirmed in decision 0035; owner sets amounts; check with first-100-orders plan so referral cost fits the CAC budget).

## 5.7 Micro-influencer seeding - fees, barter, vetting
**Real numbers (ESTIMATE; vendor blogs disagree; not home-decor-specific):**
| Tier | Followers | Typical rate (India, 2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | 1-10K | Rs 1,000-15,000 per post/reel (sources give Rs 2,000-8,000 for the middle of the band) | Best value for a fresh brand; higher trust |
| Micro | 10-100K | Rs 8,000-85,000 per deliverable; reels Rs 2,000-35,000 (one source), Rs 5,000-25,000 typical | Sources: upgrowth.in, dazzlerr.com, influencermarketinghub.com (links in 10) |
Reels cost 25-50% more than static posts; usage rights (using their content in your ads) usually cost extra (HEURISTIC). Home-decor specific rates: not found - get 3 quotes.
**Barter reality for furniture:** creators rarely accept furniture as full payment for reels. Offer barter of small pieces (landed cost to us Rs 1,500-4,000) + a modest cash fee for nano creators, and full-price-value furniture only for creators who are actually moving/renovating and have an India-based audience. Barter is still a "material connection" and must be disclosed (09).
**Suggested pilot (ASSUMPTION; align with first-100-orders budget):** 10 creators over weeks 3-8: 6 nano barter (small decor piece + Rs 0-3,000), 3 nano cash (Rs 3,000-8,000 for a reel), 1 micro (Rs 10,000-25,000). Total cash Rs 20,000-50,000 plus product cost.
**Brief (one page):** message (custom + made in Jodhpur), 3 must-say points, 1 must-show (tape measure/fit or joint macro), CTA (WhatsApp `ref:<creator>` + their code), disclosure line, posting date, usage rights 90 days, approve draft before posting, no false claims.
**Tracking:** unique WhatsApp ref, unique coupon code (0035; orders record `couponCode`), UTM `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=<handle>_<yyyymm>`.

### Vetting checklist (score each 0-2; hire >= 12/16)
1. Niche fit: home, renovation, interiors, new-home, wedding, DIY - not general lifestyle giveaways.
2. Audience geography: >= 70% India (ask for Insights screenshots dated within 30 days) and target city overlap.
3. Engagement: (avg likes + comments over last 10 posts) / followers - compare to peers; as a HEURISTIC, 2-6% is healthy for micro accounts; investigate anything <1% or >15%.
4. Comment quality: real sentences, not only emojis/"nice"; check for pods.
5. Follower growth chart: no vertical jumps (Social Blade-type tool or Insights screenshot).
6. Reels views vs followers: consistent (views >= ~30% of followers on average is a positive HEURISTIC; sudden spikes fine).
7. Past brand collabs disclosed properly (#ad / #collab) - ASCI relevance (09).
8. Professionalism: replies within 24 h, sends draft on time, clear rates, contract/email.
Red flags: won't share insights, price >3x peers with no proof, asks 100% advance for a brand-new relationship, only follower-count boasts, previous fake-follower/bot reports, promises "guaranteed sales".
Payment: nano barter = product after draft approval; cash = 50% at approval of script, 50% after live and disclosure verified (ASSUMPTION).

## 5.8 Pinterest as a decor search channel
Pinterest is a visual search engine with long-lived pins; India contributes about 7% of its web traffic (ESTIMATE, digitalapplied stats). Standard/video pins now share one Pin format (ESTIMATE). Product rich pins pull price/availability from your website metadata (developers.pinterest.com) and can be claimed by verifying the domain (help.pinterest.com).
1. Create Pinterest Business `thewoodvintage`; **claim website**; ask engineering (tracker request; no code now) to add Open Graph/product metadata for rich pins.
2. Boards (12): Sheesham furniture ideas, Bedroom ideas India, Storage beds, Small-flat living, Dining table ideas, Wooden decor, Housewarming gifts, Jaali and carved details, Wood care tips, Workshop process, Festive decor, Custom furniture ideas.
3. Pin specs: vertical 2:3 (e.g. 1000x1500), text overlay title in Cormorant, keyword-rich title and description (60-100 chars title; describe wood, room, size), alt text, link to the exact product/collection page with UTM `utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=organic_pin&utm_campaign=<board-slug>`.
4. Cadence: 5-10 pins/week (batch 30 pins in a 90-minute session); recycle each reel/carousel as 3 pins.
5. KPI: outbound clicks, saves, and site sessions from `utm_source=pinterest` (08). Expect slow compounding (weeks-months). (HEURISTIC)

## 5.9 YouTube Shorts and long video
- Upload the same reel (cleanly, without third-party watermarks) as a Short; use a keyword title ("How a sheesham bed is made in Jodhpur"). Instagram favours original content on its own surface (ESTIMATE), so post natively.
- Monthly 6-8 min "workshop tour / how we make {piece}" video for search and trust; embed on site (`ARTISAN_SPOTLIGHT`/BRAND sections from decision 0030 can host) - website change logged as a tracker item.
- UTM: `utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=shorts_organic|video_organic&utm_campaign=<slug>`.

## 5.10 Meta Groups and Facebook communities
Join 10-15 relevant Facebook groups (city groups, home-renovation, interior ideas, Rajasthan buyers, NRI groups) and read the rules. Value-first: answer questions, share care tips, post only where self-promotion is allowed (pinned "promo thread"). Never post the same link in many groups (spam, bans). Consider creating a small **Facebook Group or WhatsApp Community**: "Custom Furniture India" with weekly tips - only after the Page has content.

Basis: US = 38.7% of Indian handicraft exports (VERIFIED, IBEF); Etsy shows sheesham decor listings; hybrid model: small decor (<10 kg/piece) via Etsy/Amazon + B2B enquiries via quote capture (decision 0034); own-site US checkout not ready.
- **Instagram:** separate content angle: "Made by hand in India" + care/shipping/tariff transparency; do not run the US and India audiences through the same ads.
- **Etsy-linked Pinterest/Instagram:** Etsy sellers use Pinterest as a slow-burn traffic source and link Etsy to Instagram (ESTIMATE, Craftybase/Printful). Pin listing photos with keyword titles; pin to boards like "rustic decor", "boho wooden decor", "sheesham wood".
- **Diaspora:** Indian-American groups, Diwali content, "a piece from home" story; ask Indian friends abroad to share (referral).
- **B2B:** interior designers, boutique retailers, hospitality hosts; use the quote form + WhatsApp with US country code; LinkedIn outreach is outside this social playbook.
- **TikTok:** treat as an experiment only after Instagram/Pinterest are working; verify current US availability/ownership and business-account features (ESTIMATE/unknown as of this research).
- **Compliance:** FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255): disclose any material connection clearly and conspicuously, in the post itself, not only in the profile or behind "more" (ESTIMATE, eCFR/FTC pages). Tariff/duty facts change quickly: recheck before any US promotion (us.md).
- **KPIs:** Etsy views/favourites/orders, B2B enquiries with `utm_country=us` naming in campaign (08).

## 5.12 UAE pilot (later; see docs/countries/uae.md)
Basis: UAE = 7.68% of Indian handicraft exports (VERIFIED, IBEF); CEPA reduces duty (ESTIMATE, moet.gov.ae); research on prices/competitors is thin (uae.md).
- **Instagram-first** (the leading commerce platform in UAE per vendor reports - weak evidence). Content: Indian-expat community + interior-designer community; English captions with Arabic phrases/greetings for Ramadan/Eid; Arabic translation by a native reviewer (not machine-only).
- **Collab with Dubai/Abu Dhabi interior designers and home-decor creators:** vendor sources rank Dubai home/interior creators by follower count (influData Aug 2026 list, ESTIMATE); pick by fit, not by size.
- **Compliance:** since 1 Feb 2026 any person publishing advertising content from within the UAE (paid or unpaid, including gifted partnerships) must hold a UAE Media Council Advertiser Permit, with heavy fines for non-compliance (ESTIMATE; multiple 2026 explainers; verify with UAE counsel): https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-influencers-must-obtain-advertiser-permit-under-new-media-law/ . Check any UAE-based creator holds a valid permit before collaborating.
- **Payments/shipping/VAT** are prerequisites (uae.md); do not promote a purchase path that does not exist.
