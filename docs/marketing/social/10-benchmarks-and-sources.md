### What the benchmarks tell us (reasoned, not measured)
1. National brands (200-500K) run mostly product/lifestyle carousels and UGC and use Highlights as a mini-website (Best Sellers, Testimonials,
   Offers, Store tours). Copy the **Highlight structure**, not the catalogue-style feed.
2. The one Jodhpur manufacturer we could read (Woodsala, ~21K after years) posts product reels and shows **no visible workshop/artisan story**.
   Our differentiated slot is the making, the makers and the custom-brief story. (ASSUMPTION: gap persists - confirm by scrolling their feed.)
3. Small Indian makers (1.7K, 8K) show the failure mode: hundreds of posts, tiny audience - catalogue posting without a hook or a reason to follow.
4. The woodworking-creator benchmark (Canadian Woodworks) proves the "process + education + store" model works at scale on IG, but is not
   evidence for India furniture conversion.
5. A realistic organic target of 5-10K followers in 6-12 months is an ASSUMPTION (a Jodhpur peer sits at ~21K after years of posting). Treat follower
   count as a vanity metric; the goal is WhatsApp enquiries (see 08-measurement.md).
