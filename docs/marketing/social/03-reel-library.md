# 3. Reel library - 39 concepts with scripts, shot lists, captions, hashtags, CTAs

Part of [../social-media-playbook.md](../social-media-playbook.md). Structure = HOOK -> STORY -> PROOF -> CTA ([02-content-strategy.md](02-content-strategy.md) 2.4). Hashtag sets H1-H8 are defined in 02 (2.5).
Placeholders: `{CarpenterA}` real first name (with consent), `{N}` real number from the owner, `{number}` WhatsApp number, `{X}` real lead time. **Do not shoot or publish a claim until the owner has confirmed it is true** (wood species, seasoning time, joinery, warranty, price %). Items marked [VERIFY] are factual claims to confirm before posting.
Default specs: 1080x1920, 24/30 fps, 25-40 s, subtitles burned in, original workshop sound + optional light music from a licensed source, end card 1.5 s (wordmark + "Made in Jodhpur"). VO language: Hinglish (EN/HI alternates in brackets).
Caption format: line 1 hook+keyword; lines 2-3 story; line 4 CTA; then hashtags.

## Group C - Education and myth-busting (P3)

**R19 - Sheesham vs mango wood vs engineered wood.** Hook: "Sheesham ya aam? Sach kya hai?" Shots: three samples side by side; weight/hardness handling; end-grain close-ups; simple pros/cons text; final: "Depends on budget and use." Proof: cut ends. Script points (write only what our carpenters agree is true [VERIFY with team]): Sheesham - dense, strong, visible grain variation, higher cost; Mango - lighter and usually more affordable, good for many pieces; Engineered (MDF/plywood/particle board with laminate) - consistent look, lower cost, more sensitive to water damage and harder to repair. Caption: "Which wood for which room? A carpenter's honest answer." Tags: H3. CTA: "Send this to someone buying a bed."

**R20 - 3 ways to check if it is really solid wood.** Hook: "Kya yeh sach me solid wood hai? 3 test." Shots: check under/back for grain continuity, end-grain visible, weight difference, repeated pattern warning for laminate, ask for the species. Never demonstrate a test that damages someone else's product. Caption: "Ask the seller: which wood, which parts. We tell you exactly." Tags: H3. CTA: Save.

**R21 - Tap test: is hollow always bad?** Hook: "Table thok ke dekho - awaaz batati hai." Shots: knocking on solid vs hollow-core vs panel; explain use cases. Tags: H3. CTA: Comment "TEST".

**R22 - Monsoon care for wooden furniture.** Hook: "Barsaat me furniture kaise bachayein?" Shots: 5 tips (don't place against damp wall, use dry cloth, ventilate, avoid wet mopping near legs, polish schedule) - each 4 s. Tags: H3. CTA: Save; "Send to your parents." (Seasonal: Jun-Sep; if posting in Oct, use "Winter care" variant.)

**R23 - Why is my sheesham two-toned? (It is normal.)** Hook: "Ek hi bed me do rang? Galti nahi." Shots: heartwood/sapwood contrast, how polish evens tone, note "natural variation." Tags: H3. CTA: "Want uniform look? Ask about our finish options."

**R24 - Bed size guide in feet.** Hook: "King ya queen? Aapka room kitna bada hai?" Shots: floor tape outline in a room, person lying, walkway gaps text (e.g. keep 2 ft walkway - HEURISTIC), common mattress sizes India. Text: verify sizes before publishing. Tags: H3/H4. CTA: WhatsApp room size for a free suggestion.

**R25 - Termites and humidity: what is real?** Hook: "Kya sheesham me deemak lagta hai?" Shots: carpenter explains, treatment steps [VERIFY: do we treat?], storage tips. Avoid absolute claims like "termite-proof". Tags: H3. CTA: Ask us.

## How to use the library
- First 30 days: R01, R03, R19, R11-R14, R26, R29 are the highest-priority shoots (they use assets you already have and map to the funnel).
- Batch shoot by location: workshop (R01-R10), custom-in-progress (R11-R13, R16-R18), home-styling (R15, R30-R33 need a customer/staged room).
- Reuse: each reel can yield a carousel (slides from key frames), 3 stories, and a Pinterest video pin (see 05).
- Do not repost the same reel twice within 30 days; repackage with a new hook instead. (HEURISTIC)
