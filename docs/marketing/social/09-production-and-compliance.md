## 9.3 Lighting, audio, framing rules
- **Light:** shoot near an open shutter/window in soft morning (9-11) or late-afternoon light; no direct hard sun on the finished piece; no mixed colour lights (turn off tube lights when using LED); lock white balance; bounce with the reflector. (HEURISTIC)
- **Audio:** voice = lav mic in a quieter corner; workshop tool sound = record separately close to the source (ASMR value); record 30 s of room tone; if the shop is too loud record voiceover afterwards in a quiet room with a blanket over you and add subtitles.
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16; head-room; hands and tools in frame; one shot type per beat: wide, medium, macro detail. 20 clips of 3-6 s beat one 60 s take.
- **Scale and reality:** put a person, tape measure or standard object in the shot; unstaged dust/wood shavings are on-brand, but keep the frame tidy.
- **Safety and optics:** film workers wearing PPE where relevant (mask/goggles), no minors on the shop floor in any footage, no unsafe machine handling in frame.

## 9.4 Shot bible (capture for every product)
Hero wide in a room; 45-degree; front; side; 3 details (grain, joint, hardware/finish); scale shot; 360-degree slow walk-around (10 s); drawer/door opening; top-down; close-up of price-tag-free texture; before/after where applicable; and a 15-second "what it is" spoken piece to camera. Save as `hero`, `detail`, `walk`, `scale` in the file name.

## 9.5 Editing apps and settings
- **CapCut** (mobile/desktop; free tier; verify current terms for commercial use and template/music licence - ESTIMATE): captions, speed ramps, beat cuts, transitions; import Cormorant Garamond/Noto Sans Devanagari where supported.
- **InShot:** quick trims, backgrounds; **Canva:** carousels/covers/end cards (brand kit with #3B2314/#A0693A/#FFFCF5, Cormorant).
- **Export:** 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, bitrate ~8-12 Mbps; cover frame 1080x1350 safe; keep under 100 MB.
- **Template:** 0-2 s hook text; 2-25 s scenes 1.5-4 s each; last 3 s CTA card; auto-caption then correct Hindi/English spelling manually.
- **Cut checklist:** every second earns its place; audio levels: voice -12 dB to -6 dB peak, music at 10-20% under voice; no jump-cut over important speech.

## 9.6 Batching workflow (weekly rhythm)
Mon: review analytics (08), pick next week's hooks; Tue: shoot 30-60 min in the workshop (WIP clips); Wed: edit 2 reels; Thu: edit 2 reels + 1 carousel; Fri: schedule the next week in Meta Business Suite; Sat: capture deliveries/customer moments; Sun: rest + question-box story. Daily: stories + 30-min community routine (05) + WhatsApp SLA (07).
Roles for a 1-2 person team:
| Role | Owner/Brand lead (Person A) | Content-ops (Person B or freelancer) |
|---|---|---|
| Decisions | Approves claims, prices, festival slots, giveaways, creators | Follows calendar, edits, schedules |
| Creative | Appears on camera; picks best customer stories | Shoots, edits, writes captions in Hinglish |
| Community | Handles VIP/complex DMs, quotes | Comments/DMs, moves leads to WhatsApp, updates sheets |
| Reporting | Reads weekly review; decides next tests | Builds the report (08) |
Freelancer note (ESTIMATE): local reel-editors typically charge per reel; get 2-3 quotes; contract IP ownership = brand, delivery in 48 h, correct disclosures. Do not outsource comments/DMs to an agency in the first 60 days: the brand voice and speed matter.

## 9.8 Approval checklist (two-person rule: creator + approver tick before scheduling)
- [ ] Claim check: every factual claim (wood species, "solid", seasoning time, joinery, warranty, delivery time, price %, "made in Jodhpur", sustainability/legal timber) is TRUE and evidence-backed (see 9.9).
- [ ] Hook in first 2 s; on-screen text spelled correctly; subtitles accurate (Hindi/English).
- [ ] Safe zones respected; cover and alt text set; location tag Jodhpur.
- [ ] CTA present and single; link/UTM correct (08); WhatsApp `ref:` code included.
- [ ] Hashtags 3-5 from the right set (02); no banned/irrelevant tags.
- [ ] Music licensed for commercial use, or original sound only.
- [ ] People/faces/homes: consent on file; children and number plates blurred; no customer phone/address visible.
- [ ] No competitor logos/names/copyrighted images; no misleading before/after; no fake urgency/stock claims.
- [ ] If collaborating: creator disclosure label present and visible before "more" (9.10).
- [ ] Sensitive festival/religious content reviewed for respect.
- [ ] Scheduled in the tracker with pillar tag.

## 9.9 Compliance and IP checklist
1. **Claims must be true (highest risk).**
   - Species and grade: "sheesham" (Dalbergia sissoo), "mango wood", "teak" (Tectona grandis), "solid" (all main components?), "veneer", "engineered". If a piece uses plywood/MDF in places, say so; "solid teak" only if verified by species and part. Keep supplier invoices and photos.
   - "Handmade/handcrafted": be precise ("hand-finished", "made in our workshop with hand and power tools").
   - "Made in Jodhpur/our own carpenters": true for each item shown; label bought-in items differently.
   - "30% cheaper than the market": an ASSUMPTION in the workstream plan (not measured); do not publish until documented against comparable **sale** prices (Wooden Street lists sheesham king beds at 45-62% off MRP, VERIFIED 2026-09-25, in.md). Never name competitors in comparative claims without substantiation and ASCI/Consumer law review.
   - "Lifetime warranty": only if it is written, priced-in and honoured. Some Jodhpur competitors claim it (VERIFIED as claim on their sites, competitor directory), which is not a reason for us to.
   - "Eco-friendly / sustainable / legally sourced / reclaimed": require documents (supplier, permits). Timber legality matters for export (Lacey/EUDR/CITES: see market-selection.md and us.md); sheesham items over 10 kg per item need CITES permits (us.md).
   - "Delivered in X days", "free installation": only if operationally true. Stock/urgency statements must be real (decision 0034 spec bans fake urgency).
2. **Advertising rules (India).** The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the CCPA's misleading-advertisement/endorsement guidelines target false or misleading ads and undisclosed endorsements (from general knowledge - ESTIMATE, verify current text); ASCI's code requires ads to be truthful and substantiated. For influencer/endorsement rules see 9.10. Read the ASCI code and guidelines at ascionline.in once (we could not extract the guideline text in this research).
3. **Meta ad and commerce policy basics (verify at facebook.com/policies_center):** ads must not be misleading or use exaggerated/unverifiable claims; images must be yours or licensed; landing page must match the ad; catalog items must show correct price/availability; no prohibited content; repeated violations can restrict the ad account (ESTIMATE/policy risk). Keep a screenshot of every approved ad and its claim evidence.
4. **Music licensing.** Business accounts typically have a smaller commercial music library than personal accounts; use tracks from the audio library shown in-app for business accounts, Meta Sound Collection, or licensed libraries; never use trending pop audio in **ads**; never rip music from other apps; check CapCut's template/music licence terms for commercial use (ESTIMATE - verify). Prefer original workshop sound.
5. **Model releases (carpenters and staff).** Get a one-page consent in Hindi + English (WhatsApp photo of signed form is fine for small teams) covering: name, right to use image/voice/video on social media, website, ads and packaging; duration ({2} years) and territory (worldwide); withdrawal right (stop future use on written request); no minors; voluntary; any reward. Keep in `06_consents`. Treat photos of employees as personal data (DPDP Act, 2023 - India; ESTIMATE, verify requirements and phase-in). Respect refusal.
6. **Customer content and privacy.** Ask permission before reposting; blur addresses, number plates, children's faces; do not reveal customer phone numbers; keep lead data only for the stated purpose (decision 0034 notes the privacy policy must mention leads and deletion is manual); honour deletion requests.
7. **Influencer disclosure (India).** ASCI's influencer guidelines require disclosing a material connection - paid, barter/gifted, affiliate - and labels reported as "#ad", "#collaboration"/"#collab", "#sponsored", "#partnership", "#promo", placed upfront (before the caption "more"), and in video within the first seconds plus an overlay (secondary sources; ESTIMATE; the primary page did not load text). Generic labels like "#gifted" or "#partner" reportedly do not suffice. Brand and influencer share responsibility; write it in the contract; use Instagram's **Paid partnership label** toggle when available, plus a text disclosure. Ask influencers to review only products they've genuinely used and to make no claims beyond your approved list.
   Template lines: caption line 1 `#ad Gifted by The Wood Vintage` (check label list); on-screen text "Paid partnership with @thewoodvintage" for the first 5 s.
8. **Giveaways/contests:** merit-based entry, published terms, no purchase, platform rules, tax check with CA (05 5.3).
9. **Copyright and design IP.** Use only own footage, licensed music, licensed fonts (Cormorant is open licence via Google Fonts - ESTIMATE, confirm), properly licensed stock; the homepage currently uses licence-attributed photography (decision 0030 `ATTRIBUTION.json`) - do not present those stock images as our workshop on social. Do not copy competitors' product designs or images; if customers bring a photo of another brand's design, build a differentiated design rather than an exact copy (competitor directory rule "study strengths, don't imitate"). Consider registering original design(s) later (ASSUMPTION - legal advice).
10. **Testimonials and reviews:** only from genuine customers; never write or buy reviews; do not selectively hide all negatives; edit only for spelling with permission.
11. **Religious/cultural sensitivity:** respectful festival content; no religious symbols used as decorative mockery; mandir pieces treated with respect.
12. **Worker welfare optics:** PPE, no minors, fair-work language; never imply "cheap labour" as the reason for low prices.
13. **USA:** FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) - disclose material connections clearly and conspicuously in the post, not only on the profile or behind "more" (ESTIMATE, eCFR/FTC). **UAE:** since 1 Feb 2026 anyone publishing advertising content in the UAE (paid or unpaid, gifted included) needs a UAE Media Council Advertiser Permit (ESTIMATE, multiple 2026 explainers; verify with UAE counsel).
14. **Record-keeping:** keep for 3 years: consents, claim evidence, ad screenshots, influencer contracts/payments, contest terms and winner proof.
