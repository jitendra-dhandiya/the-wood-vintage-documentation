# 2. Content strategy - pillars, formats, reel structure, hashtags, Hinglish, stories

Part of [../social-media-playbook.md](../social-media-playbook.md). Reel scripts are in [03-reel-library.md](03-reel-library.md); day-by-day placement in [04-calendar-and-roadmap.md](04-calendar-and-roadmap.md).
Tags: HEURISTIC = best-practice rule of thumb, not a platform rule. Nothing here is a claim that a format "will" go viral.

## 2.1 The strategic bet (why content looks like this)
- **Moat = the workshop.** Wooden Street, Pepperfry and Urban Ladder are showroom/catalogue brands; the one Jodhpur maker we read (Woodsala) shows product reels but no visible maker story (VERIFIED 2026-09-25, see 10-benchmarks-and-sources.md). We own "the making, the makers, and made-to-your-size".
- **Goal of the account is not followers; it is WhatsApp enquiries -> quotes -> advance payments.** Every pillar has a CTA into WhatsApp/quote form (decision 0034: primary CTA "Get Best Quote & Price", strong secondary "Chat on WhatsApp").
- **Discovery levers (as reported):** watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach are the ranking signals Mosseri named (ESTIMATE, secondary). So design for: (1) people watching to the end, (2) people **sending** it to a spouse/parent/designer ("send this to whoever is buying your bed"). Every reel should have one "send-worthy" line. (HEURISTIC)

## 2.4 The reel structure: HOOK -> STORY -> PROOF -> CTA
Use it as a checklist on every reel; timings for a 30-40 s reel (HEURISTIC):

| Block | Time | Job | Example (bed build) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HOOK** | 0-2 s | Stop the thumb: a question, a surprising number, or a visually striking shot + on-screen text (<= 6 words). Must make sense with sound off. | "This bed took 6 days to build." over a rough plank vs finished headboard split-screen |
| **STORY** | 2-22 s | 3-5 fast beats (1.5-4 s each) that pay the hook: the problem, the process, the twist. One idea per reel. | Cutting -> joinery -> sanding -> polish -> in-room |
| **PROOF** | 22-32 s | A moment that a sceptic believes: a real name, a measurement, the grain close-up, a customer reaction, the tape measure. | Carpenter says "Yeh mortise-tenon hai, koi keel nahi" + macro shot of joint |
| **CTA** | 32-38 s | One ask, spoken and on screen: comment a keyword, WhatsApp, save, send. | "Apne size ka bed? WhatsApp 'BED' - sketch + price milega." |
Loop trick (HEURISTIC): make the last frame flow into the first so replays raise watch time.
Length rule: never pad; cut every shot that does not move the story (Instagram rewards watch time, ESTIMATE).
On-screen text: max 2 lines at a time; text stays 1.5 s minimum; Hindi and English versions of the same line alternate only if legible.

### Comment-keyword CTA (funnel)
"Comment BED / SOFA / SIZE and we'll message you" - manual reply in the first 30 days (see 07); automate with a tool only after checking Meta's automation rules and using opt-in. Every keyword maps to a WhatsApp quick reply.

## 2.5 Hashtag and keyword system
Instagram now recommends 3-5 relevant hashtags, and hashtags help search, not reach (Mosseri, reported Dec 2025 - ESTIMATE, https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/). So: **put the keywords in the caption's first line and in on-screen text; add 3-5 hashtags.** Verify volumes yourself in-app; we could not pull tag counts.
Use one brand tag + 1-2 niche + 1-2 broad/local. Sets (rotate; each <= 5):

| Set | Use for | Tags |
|---|---|---|
| H1 Workshop | P1 process | `#thewoodvintage #jodhpurfurniture #carpentry #handcrafted #madeinjodhpur` |
| H2 Custom | P2 | `#thewoodvintage #customfurniture #madetoorder #furnituredesign #woodenfurniture` |
| H3 Wood education | P3 | `#thewoodvintage #sheeshamwood #woodenfurniture #woodworking #furniturebuyingguide` |
| H4 Real homes | P4 | `#thewoodvintage #indianhomedecor #homedecorindia #interiorindia #housewarming` |
| H5 Pricing/buying | P5 | `#thewoodvintage #furnitureshopping #factorydirect #homeinteriors #newhome` |
| H6 Festive | P6 | `#thewoodvintage #diwalidecor #festivehome #diwali2026 #homemakeover` |
| H7 Wedding/gift | P6 | `#thewoodvintage #weddinggift #shaadigift #newhomegift #indianwedding` (brand-safety: never use dowry framing or tags) |
| H8 US/UAE later | export | `#thewoodvintage #handmadefurniture #sheeshamwood #rusticdecor #indianfurniture` |
Caption keyword pattern (searchable phrases, HEURISTIC): "sheesham wood bed made in Jodhpur", "custom sofa set India", "solid wood dining table 6 seater", "wooden furniture for small flats", "housewarming gift ideas wooden". Add city/state names people actually search: Jodhpur, Jaipur, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai - only where you deliver.
Reels SEO: say the keyword aloud in the first 3 s and show it as text (Instagram reads captions, text and audio - ESTIMATE); set cover text + alt text; add location Jodhpur; use a descriptive first caption line, not "Wait for it".

### Trending audio: usage rules
1. Business accounts have a smaller licensed-music library than personal accounts; use tracks shown in the Reels audio tab for your account or Meta's Sound Collection (ESTIMATE - check in-app).
2. Do not use audio ripped from other videos/apps for commercial posts or ads; prefer original workshop sound + light voiceover.
3. A trending audio is a bonus, not the plan: use it only if it fits the video's story (a "before/after" fits most). Set the trending track quietly under original sound so speech stays clear. (HEURISTIC)
4. Never use a trending audio in a paid ad unless the licence covers ads (many do not - see 09).

## 2.6 Hinglish / Hindi caption guidance
- **Script:** Roman Hinglish for reach and search ("Yeh bed 6 din me bana"); Devanagari for festivals, family and Hindi-only audiences. Keep the on-screen hook in the language of the target viewer; A/B by pillar (measure retention).
- **Structure (caption):** Line 1 = hook + keyword (<= 125 chars so it shows before "more"). Lines 2-4 = story in 2-3 short sentences. Line 5 = CTA. Line 6 = 3-5 hashtags.
- **Tone:** aap-form, short sentences, no heavy Sanskrit ("shubh awsar" only in festival posts), simple numbers ("6 feet x 6.5 feet"), units in feet/inches AND cm for India+ export.
- **Words that work as hooks (HEURISTIC):** "Pata hai?", "Sach ya jhooth?", "Yeh galti mat karna", "Kitne ka banta hai?", "Ek din ki kahani", "Delivery se pehle dekhiye".
- **Avoid:** machine-translated Hindi; mixing three languages in one sentence; dowry/gendered framing; caste/regional stereotyping; comparing with named competitors.
- **Copy examples:**
  - EN: "Solid sheesham or mango wood for your bed? Here is what our carpenters actually use and why."
  - HI: "आपके बेड के लिए शीशम बेहतर है या आम की लकड़ी? हमारे कारीगर क्या इस्तेमाल करते हैं, देखिए।"
  - Hinglish: "Sheesham ya aam ki lakdi? Hamare carpenters kya use karte hain, dekhiye."
  - CTA Hinglish: "Apne room ka size bhejiye, hum sketch aur price bhejenge. WhatsApp: {number}"

## 2.7 Story sequences (copy-paste frame plans)
Interactive sticker in at least every second frame (poll, quiz, emoji slider, question box). Link sticker to the quote form with `utm_medium=story` (see 08).

1. **Meet the team (Day 1-3 of launch)** - F1 "Namaste from Jodhpur" workshop pan; F2 owner intro (10 s); F3 carpenter A hands close-up + name; F4 carpenter B; F5 wood yard; F6 poll "Sheesham or Mango?"; F7 "Follow to watch us build" + link to Highlight Start Here.
2. **Order diary (5 stories over 5 days for one custom order)** - Day 1 brief screenshot (name hidden) + poll "Guess the wood"; Day 2 cutting; Day 3 assembly + sticker "Slider: how strong?"; Day 4 sanding/polish; Day 5 packing + "Where would you put it?" question box. Save to Custom Highlight.
3. **Ask the carpenter (weekly)** - F1 question box "Ask our carpenter anything about wood/furniture"; F2-F5 answers on video (15 s each); F6 "Send your own room size for a free sketch" CTA.
4. **Behind the price** - F1 "Kitne ka banta hai ek bed?"; F2 wood cost (owner shares a real % split); F3 labour; F4 finishing; F5 transport; F6 "no showroom rent, no middleman"; F7 quote CTA. (Publish only with real numbers - owner input.)
5. **This or That (engagement)** - polls: walnut vs honey finish; storage bed vs plain; jaali vs plain headboard; cane vs solid panel.
6. **Festive build countdown (Oct-Nov)** - "12 din baaki Diwali me", showing pieces being finished for delivery; real "last order date for Diwali delivery: {date}" sticker.
7. **Delivery day** - truck loading, packing, "installed" reaction, review request soft-ask.
8. **Quiz** - "Which is sheesham?" swipe quiz using two samples; reveal tomorrow (creates return visits).
