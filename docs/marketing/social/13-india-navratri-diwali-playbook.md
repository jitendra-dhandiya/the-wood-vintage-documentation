## 0. Honesty rules for every line below (do not edit these away)
These are the same non-negotiables as the US doc, applied to India:
- **Never claim ready-made Diwali stock that does not exist.** If a hero piece is a concept, sketch, or WIP, label it "in progress" / "coming for Diwali" / "design concept" — never "in stock" or "ready to ship" until it truly is.
- **Wood claims:** say "sheesham", "mango wood", "teak" etc. only for pieces really made of that species (per `positioning-and-usp.md` §6 and `social/09` §9.9); "solid wood" only if solid, not MDF/plywood. Never say "teak" for sheesham/mango.
- **No fake urgency, no fake stock counters, no invented review counts.** The only real urgency allowed is a genuine last-order-date for Diwali delivery, computed by the workshop (build days + transit + buffer) — see §5 note and §6 DW08. Until the owner/workshop confirms that date, every urgency line below is marked `[PLACEHOLDER — confirm with workshop]`.
- **"30% cheaper" / discount claims:** do not use unless measured against real comparable selling prices (positioning doc §4, §6).
- **Carpenter/customer consent:** no one appears on camera without signed consent on file (`social/09` §9.9.5); no minors in any shot.
- **WhatsApp number:** `whatsapp_number` in Admin > Settings > Leads is still the placeholder `919876543200`/`919876543210` (decision 0034) — every link/script below uses `{WHATSAPP_NUMBER}`; replace before anything goes live.
- **GST/pricing:** show GST-inclusive prices (how the Indian market already displays them, positioning doc §5); say "GST invoice provided" only once Razorpay/GST invoicing is actually live in production.

## 7. WhatsApp broadcast templates

**Compliance note (brief, not full legal doc):** WhatsApp Business broadcast lists only reach contacts who
have messaged you first or otherwise opted in (WhatsApp Business policy — ESTIMATE, platform ToS); never
broadcast to a purchased/scraped number list. Under India's **DPDP Act, 2023** (ASSUMPTION/ESTIMATE — not
independently verified in this session, legal review recommended before scaling broadcasts), a phone number
is personal data: collect it with a clear stated purpose (replying to an enquiry, sending order updates),
let people opt out, and do not reuse enquiry numbers for unrelated marketing without consent. **TRAI's DND
(Do Not Disturb)/TCCCPR rules** primarily govern commercial calls/SMS via registered telemarketers
(ESTIMATE) — WhatsApp broadcast marketing is a materially different channel, but the safe practice either
way is: message only people who messaged you or ticked an opt-in box, include a one-line opt-out ("reply
STOP to not receive updates"), and honour opt-outs immediately. Keep opt-in proof per `social/09` §9.9.6.
